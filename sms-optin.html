SMS Opt-In Disclosure

Mejor Vida Insurance LLC sends SMS text messages to consumers who provide express written consent on our website. This page describes our SMS program, how you opt in, and your rights. Nebraska Department of Insurance producer license #21695431.

Program Description

Our SMS program provides informational messages about final expense and life insurance options, including personalized quotes, appointment reminders, application follow-up, and policy-related updates. Messages are sent from our SMS number 402-844-1199.

How You Opt In

You may opt in by providing your mobile phone number and optionally checking the SMS consent box on one of our online quote forms before you submit. The checkbox is not required to submit the form or receive a quote. Express written SMS consent is recorded only when you check the box and submit. Consent is not a condition of purchasing insurance where prohibited by law.

  • Free online quote (Nebraska): https://www.mejorvidainsurance.com/quote.html
  • Spanish-language landing quote: https://www.mejorvidainsurance.com/landing-gastos-finales.html
  • Online quote: https://www.mejorvidainsurance.com/quote.html

Message Frequency

1–5 SMS messages per week during active quoting and follow-up, as needed. Frequency may decrease after your request is complete or you opt out.

How to Opt Out

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier. Mejor Vida Insurance LLC does not charge a separate fee for SMS.

Privacy of Phone Numbers

We handle mobile numbers with safeguards consistent with our Privacy Policy. We do not sell your phone number and do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

TCPA and Regulatory Compliance

Mejor Vida Insurance LLC complies with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and applicable Nebraska regulations for SMS communications. We use Telnyx or other authorized providers to deliver SMS where applicable.
