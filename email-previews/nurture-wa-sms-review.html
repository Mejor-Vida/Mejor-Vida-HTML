Phase 1 — WhatsApp

Implementation

The cron calls ManyChat sendFlow with MANYCHAT_FLOW_PHASE1_STEP1 and MANYCHAT_FLOW_PHASE1_STEP2 (flow namespace per step). The actual user-facing text, buttons, and media are edited inside ManyChat, not in Git.

To review or change WhatsApp nurture copy: open ManyChat → the flows referenced by those env vars (same names as in .env / Vercel).

Intent (from code comments)

  • Step 1 (≈5 hr): value + book call
  • Step 2 (≈21 hr): soft check-in

Phase 2 — SMS (exact strings)

Hi Maria! This is Julie from Mejor Vida Insurance. You recently asked about final expense coverage — reply QUOTE and I'll send you a free quote link, or reply CALL to schedule a quick chat with me. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Hey Maria, Julie here from Mejor Vida Insurance! Final expense plans start under $30/month — could be a perfect fit. Save my contact so I'm just a tap away 👉 https://www.mejorvidainsurance.com/julie.vcf — then reply QUOTE or CALL. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
