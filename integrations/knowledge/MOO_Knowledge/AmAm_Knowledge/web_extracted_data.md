### Juvenile Applications Guidelines
- If grandparent or legal guardian applies, guardianship documents required
- All children ages 0-17 within the family should be equally insured
- Do NOT insure juveniles ages 0-17 for more than their parents or legal guardians
- Parents/Legal Guardians MUST have life coverage in force when applying for coverage on children
- Face amounts should not exceed coverage on parents
- Juvenile Questionnaire Form 9825 required

---

### MVR (Motor Vehicle Report) Guidelines
MVR may be requested on life or health applicants with history of:
1. Excessive use of alcohol
2. Excessive use of prescription drugs or any use of illegal drugs
3. Admitted DWI or DUI within the last three years
4. Admitted accidents, reckless driving, or multiple speeding tickets within a year

**NOTE:** A Motor Vehicle Report WILL be ordered on all plans when applying for Accidental Death Benefit (ADB). Underwriting reserves the right to request MVR when deemed necessary.

## SOCIAL SECURITY DRAFTING
For clients who want premium drafts to coincide with Social Security payment schedule:
- Answer "Yes" to the Social Security drafting question on application
- Requested Draft Day options:
  - "1S" = payments received on the 1st
  - "3S" = payments received on the 3rd
  - "2W" = payments on 2nd Wednesday
  - "3W" = payments on 3rd Wednesday
  - "4W" = payments on 4th Wednesday
- Policy Date Request: Enter "Upon Approval"

**If bank draft returned (NSF):** Will wait until next Social Security date in following month, then draft for 2 months of premium.

## BANK ACCOUNT VERIFICATION (GIACT)
All Mobile applications using bank draft as payment method go through GIACT verification.
- Pass: "Verification Successful" — do not validate again unless new bank info provided
- Fail: "Unable to validate payment information" — can retry 2 more times (3 total attempts)
- After 3rd attempt, last entered bank info is used
