## TERM LIFE INSURANCE

### Term Life Answers (TLA) — Full Medical Underwriting

**Purpose:** Best for clients who want the lowest possible premiums and qualify for preferred rates.

**Issue Ages:**
- Term 10: Nontobacco 18-80 / Tobacco 18-75
- Term 15: Nontobacco 18-74 / Tobacco 18-70
- Term 20: Nontobacco 18-68 / Tobacco 18-65
- Term 30: Nontobacco 18-55 / Tobacco 18-50

**Face Amounts:** $100,000-$249,999 | $250,000-$499,999 | $500,000-$999,999 | $1,000,000+

**Underwriting Classes:** Preferred Plus NT | Preferred NT | Standard Plus NT | Standard NT | Substandard NT | Preferred T | Standard T | Substandard T

**Table Rates:** Tables 1(A) to 16(P) — 25% per table; table rated cases increase the fully commissionable premium

**Premium Modes:** Annual (1.00) | Semi (0.52) | Quarterly (0.265) | Monthly BSP (0.086)

**Policy Fee:** $62.50/year (commissionable for face amounts under $250,000)

**Riders:**
- Accelerated Death Benefit for Terminal Illness Rider
- Accidental Death Benefit Rider
- Disability Waiver of Premium
- Dependent Children's Rider
- Other Insured Rider
- Waiver of Premium for Unemployment Rider

**Conversion Period (policies issued April 1, 2020 or later):**
- Term 10: For ages 18-72: earlier of 10 years or age 75; ages 73+: first 2 years only
- Term 15: Earlier of 15 years or age 75
- Term 20: Earlier of 20 years or age 75
- Term 30: During first 20 policy years only

**Renewal:** Automatically renews annually after term to age 95 without evidence of insurability

## OBJECTION HANDLING

**"Too expensive"** → Move to simplified issue (TLE instead of TLA) | Lower face amount | Living Promise for final expense only | Guaranteed ADvantage as last resort

**"Not healthy enough"** → Simplified issue (TLE, IUL Express, Living Promise) | Graded benefit Living Promise for serious conditions | Guaranteed ADvantage for truly uninsurable (accidental only)

**"Already have work coverage"** → Group coverage not portable | Consider supplementing with Critical Illness or Accidental Death | Children's Whole Life as add-on

**"Don't need that much"** → Living Promise starts at $2,000 | Children's Whole Life starts at $5,000
