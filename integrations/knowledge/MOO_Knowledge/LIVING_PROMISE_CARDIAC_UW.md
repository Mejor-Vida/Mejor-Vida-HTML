## What the product is (not a class chart)

Living Promise Level / Graded is **yes/no application routing**, not Premier/Select/Preferred:

| Application answers | Result |
|---------------------|--------|
| All underwriting questions **NO** | Eligible for **Level** |
| Any **Part Two** YES | **Graded only** |
| Any **Part One** YES | May **not** be eligible for any coverage on this application |

Level classes: Standard Tobacco / Nontobacco. Graded: Standard, no tobacco distinction.

## Cardiac items on the “common impairments” list

May adjust or decline (guide; not all-inclusive): irregular heart rhythm, cardiomyopathy, CHF, coronary disease including heart attack or heart surgery, defibrillator, heart disease or surgery, pacemaker.
