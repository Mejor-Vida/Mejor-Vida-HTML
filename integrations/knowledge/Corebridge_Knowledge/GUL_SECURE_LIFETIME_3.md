# Corebridge — Secure Lifetime GUL 3

**Source:** AGLC109164 REV0824 (FP brochure)  
**File:** `raw/pdfs/AGLC109164-Secure-Lifetime-GUL-3-FP-Brochure.pdf`  
**In scope:** Yes (GUL / permanent protection). Not an IUL.  
**Forms:** ICC15-15442 / 15442 / 15442N Rev1023 (+ riders listed on brochure)

## Snapshot
- Flexible-premium UL with **secondary / continuation guarantee** (DB guarantee even if CSV hits zero if CG rules + required premiums met)
- Owner selects guarantee period (year or age) and can fund over life, shorten pay, or pre-fund
- **Guaranteed minimum cash value**; accessing CV can affect continuation guarantee
- **Enhanced Surrender Value / ROP rider:** 50% ROP end of year 20 or 100% end of year 25, capped at **40%** of lowest specified amount
- **24-month rolling target** (not NY)
- Issue ages **18–80**; max issue age 80 all classes
- Min DB **$100,000** (restrictions); NY: $50K Standard; Preferred $50K only ages 50–80
- Death benefit option: **Level** (specified amount)
- Increases anytime w/ E of I (voids guaranteed CV provision); decreases after year 1
- Premium load: current **20%** of premiums (max 20%); monthly expense per $1,000 varies
- Surrender charges: decreasing **19-year** schedule
- Loans: variable rate (Moody’s-linked); preferred loans after 10 years

## Living benefit / income riders (state availability varies — see AGLC110190)
- **Accelerated Access Solution®** — chronic illness; monthly or lump sum access to DB  
- **Lifestyle Income Solution®** — convert DB to income stream (typically tax-free up to premiums); **not MA, MO, PA** per state sheet  
- Also on portfolio: ADB, Child Term, Waiver of Monthly Deduction, Terminal Illness, Enhanced Surrender Value / ROP  

## Free look / grace (Julie states — AGLC112585)
- Free look: **10** days (**30** if replacement)  
- Grace: **61** days
