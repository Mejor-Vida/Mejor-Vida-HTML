## Definition & timing
- Contractual right to convert term → permanent of company’s choosing **without evidence of insurability** during the conversion period.
- Expiration is **product-specific** — confirm via Producer Care / `conversioneligibility@corebridgefinancial.com` (expect ~24h).
- **No grace** past conversion expiry. Home office must receive by expiry date:
  1. Signed/dated Term Conversion Request Form (**AGLC101672-PL**) or state-specific Inforce Change Form, **and**
  2. Signed/dated illustration generated **on or before** expiry  
  Both must arrive **on or before** expiry — **no exceptions**.

## Permanent products by term year (FAQ Q10)

| Term plan | Policy years | Available permanent products |
|-----------|--------------|------------------------------|
| **10-year term** | Years 1–8 | Full permanent portfolio* |
| | Years 9–10 | Protection Extend IUL, **American Elite WL 2**, American Elite 2 |
| **15-year or longer** | Years 1–10 | Full permanent portfolio* |
| | Years 11+ | Protection Extend IUL, **American Elite WL 2**, American Elite 2 |

\*Full portfolio (excl. survivorship), **all states except NY:** Max Acc+ III, Value+ Protector III, **Secure Lifetime GUL 3**, Protection Extend IUL, **American Elite WL 2**.  
**NY only:** Max Acc+ II, Value+ Protector III, Secure Lifetime GUL 3, Protection Extend IUL, American Elite 2.

## Required paperwork (typical)
- Term Conversion Request Form (no UW) **AGLC101672-PL**, or state Inforce Change App if UW needed  
- Signed illustration (agent + owner; gen’d ≤ expiry; signed ≤ app date)  
- Possible: bank draft auth, TIR disclosure, over-age / CA 65+ forms, business/trust certs, payor auth, state forms  
- Index conversions need supplemental app (Julie: avoid IUL path)

## Effective date
- Must match **day of month** of original term issue (e.g. issued 7/13 → conversion effective on a 13th)  
- Usually the **paid-to date** of the term  
- Cannot issue before conversion effective date  
- Leave “Conversion Effective Date” blank on form — company sets to anniversary alignment  

## Ops tips
- Tip sheet: know expiry, min face if term stays inforce, UW class/tables, DOB, paid-to, billing/bank, riders, owner  
- APL only on American Elite WL 2 / Elite 2  
- Issue state = where app signed; agent must be licensed there  
- Combine multiple terms: same insured, owner, UW class  
- Partial conversion: remaining term must stay ≥ plan minimum  
- Waiting period: **1 month** unless contract longer  
- Term &lt; 18 months: upper management best-interest review  
- Premium waiver on term: can convert but **client pays** new premium; waiver does **not** transfer  
- TIR exercised: face reduced by lien; TIR does **not** transfer  
- ROP term CV: cash out or roll in (written request); no face decrease on ROP term — partial conversion terminates remainder  
- IGO turnaround: ~1 week or less  

## AAS Chronic Illness on conversion (Select-a-Term only)
Available if: Select-a-Term issued within last **5 years**; convert to GUL 3 / Max Acc+ III / Value+ III; new issue age **≤60**; original **standard or better** (no substd/flat); face within AAS mins/maxes; partial OK if remaining term ≥ **$100K**; noninvasive UW for AAS (can decline AAS but still convert base); must also issue Terminal Illness Rider; Inforce Change App + Chronic Illness supplemental app. AAS has **2-year contestable** period.
