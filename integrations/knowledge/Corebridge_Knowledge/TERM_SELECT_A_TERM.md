# Corebridge — Select-a-Term (Product Highlights)

**Source:** AGLC104704 REV0226  
**File:** `raw/pdfs/AGLC104704-Select-a-Term-Product-Highlights.pdf`  
**In scope:** Yes (Term). Quote via Rapid Rater / approved quote engines — **nonillustratable** (guaranteed premiums only).

## Product snapshot
- Level term with face change after level-premium period; renewable annually after level period to expiry **age 95 ANB**
- **Min face:** $100,000  
- **Bands:** $100K–$249,999 / $250K–$499,999 / $500K–$999,999 / $1M+  
- **Policy fee:** Band 1 **$74** (commissionable); Bands 2–4 **$64** (noncommissionable)  
- **Forms:** AGL ICC21-19311/19311, ICC21-19310/19310; US Life 19310N-33 / 19311N-33  
- **Classes:** Pref Plus, Pref NT, Std Plus, Std NT, Pref T, Std T, Special NT, Special T  
- **Substandard:** ≤70 through Table H; >70 through Table D (on Special rates)

## Agile Underwriting+ (AU+)
- Ages **20–59**, faces **$100,000–$1,000,000**, digital submission  
- Classes Std → Pref Plus via AU+; see full guidelines **AGLC110667-LB**  
- If total AGL inforce + applied-for **> $1M** → exam/labs required  
- Post-issue review / material non-disclosure may rescind  

## Conversion
- Convert to AGL/US Life UL during conversion window **without evidence of insurability**  
- 10-year term: through month **96** (before 8th anniversary)  
- Terms >10 years: through month **120** (before 10th anniversary)  
- After those windows: **Protection Extend IUL** (min $50K) or **American Elite Whole Life 2**  
- Convertibility ends earlier of end of level period or insured age **70**  
- Note: Protection Extend IUL is conversion-path only — Julie does **not** sell IUL as a retail product; Elite WL 2 is the non-IUL conversion option

## Riders (state variations)
- Accidental Death (max lesser of $250K or base face; through Table D; expires ~age 70; issue only)  
- Child Rider ($1K–$25K to age 25)  
- Terminal Illness ABR — **no extra premium**; currently **50%** of specified amount (cap on schedule); 24 months to live (12 in NY); admin fee ≤ $500  
- Waiver of Premium — 6-mo wait; not if face > $5M/life; through Table D; issue only / can drop later
