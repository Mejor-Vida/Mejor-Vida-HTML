## Eagle Select underwriting

Process: demographics → product (starts at top tier) → initial medical questions (not all are knockouts) → medical authorization (MIB, Rx, medical, other third parties) → preliminary offer in minutes: **Eagle Select 1, 2, 3, or Decline** → possible extra drill-down questions → beneficiary/banking → signatures → issue.

**Not all medical questions are knockouts.** Extra questions after third-party data are to price the best offer. If none of a “check all that apply” list applies, leave blank.

### Conditions that block Eagle Select 1 (route to ES2 or decline)

From agent guide (not exhaustive):

- Respiratory **Yes** + nicotine (admit or third-party) → best class **ES2 Nicotine**.
- Stroke/TIA **Yes** + nicotine → decline ES1; best **ES2 Nicotine**.
- Heart disease **Yes** + nicotine → best **ES2 Nicotine**.
- Any **two of** heart disease, diabetes, stroke/TIA, **and** non-nicotine → best **ES2 Non-nicotine**.
- Peripheral vascular disease **and** diabetes → best **ES2**.
- PVD **Yes** + nicotine → best **ES2 Nicotine**.
- Single diabetes complication → **ES2 Nicotine**.
- Smoking with diabetes, heart disease, or respiratory disease → **ES2 Smoker**.

### Eagle Select knock-out / decline list

Knock-outs (agent guide; UW flyer omits liver/leukemia — prefer the **agent guide** list):

- Organ/tissue transplant
- Multiple sclerosis
- Systemic lupus
- ALS (Lou Gehrig’s)
- Alzheimer’s or dementia
- Huntington’s
- Brain tumor
- Parkinson’s
- Amputation due to disease
- Liver disease
- Leukemia

Declinable if **in the last 12 months**:

- Assistance with ADLs (bathing, toileting, dressing) due to debilitating disease or bed-bound
- Hospice
- Supplemental oxygen for breathing
- Dependent on wheelchair or motorized mobility device
- Advised to undergo tests, surgery, or hospitalization (excluding HIV/AIDS-related) not yet completed; awaiting diagnosis/test results; or currently hospitalized

Lists are **not exhaustive**. Combinations of conditions can still decline.

## Instant Decision IUL underwriting

- Decisions from application medical questions + **MIB** + **Rx**. No paramed, blood, urine, or APS in the standard path. Non-medical **$50,000–$450,000**, no saliva.
- **All medical questions are knock-out** on the IUL app — read the entire question. Questions 1a/b nicotine history: any nicotine checkbox → quote nicotine; “no nicotine products” → non-nicotine.
- Questions 4–13 / Section 8: if the stem is **No**, skip drill-downs; if **Yes**, complete all drill-downs.
- Not available **substandard**.
- Build chart (IUL / Term column on eApp QR), examples: 5'0" **91–216**; 5'6" **110–262**; 6'0" **131–312**; 6'7" **157–376**.

## Instant Decision Term underwriting

Accept/Reject from app questions + Rx + MIB + prior Americo apps. Non-medical through $450,000. Same Term/IUL build column as IUL (see eApp QR). ADB product uses **personal history** questions rather than full medical (glance footnote).

DIR (Disability Income Rider) has a separate, tighter build column on the eApp QR and a dedicated **DIR Occupations Guidelines** PDF (`DIR_Occupations_Guidelines_20166`). Railroad employees and military members are called out as not available for some DI features — check that PDF before adding DI.
