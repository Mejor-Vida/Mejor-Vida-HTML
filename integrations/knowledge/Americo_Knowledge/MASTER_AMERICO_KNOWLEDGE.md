### Eagle Select Quit Smoking Advantage

Available on **Eagle Select 1 and 2 nicotine** only. Quote as **nicotine**; policy issues as nicotine but the insured pays **non-nicotine rates for policy years 1–3**.

Before the third anniversary the owner chooses:

- **Option A (default):** face decreases in years 4+; premium stays level.
- **Option B:** face stays level; premium increases in years 4+ (nicotine rate at issue age).
- **Option C:** face and premium stay level — insured must attest + prove no nicotine for 12 consecutive months.

Earliest verification: after the **first** policy anniversary, with 12 consecutive nicotine-free months. Hypothetical agent-guide example (not a consumer quote): male 65, $10,000 ES1, $54.15/mo years 1–3; if never quits, default Option A ≈ $5,423.70 face at same premium, or Option B $96.63 to keep $10,000.

### Eagle Select included / optional riders

- **Accelerated Benefit Payment Rider 2146** (ES1 & 2): terminal illness with life expectancy 12 months or less (24 months in IL, MA, TX). Advance a portion of DB; max **$20,000**; min $1,000; admin fee up to **$250** + lien + interest; one acceleration per policy.
- **ADB 2179** (ES1 & 2): extra face if death from accident; additional amount if fare-paying passenger on a common carrier.
- **Child and Grandchild Term 2194:** units of $1,000, max lesser of **$5,000** or base face, same amount all children; **$2.10 per $1,000 per child annually**; child 15 days–under 17 at app; max 10 children; expires age 25; cannot add after issue except newly eligible child within 60 days. Conversion after earlier of child’s 18th birthday or base insured’s death, no later than child’s 22nd.

### CBO 100 and CBO 50

Universal life with Enhanced Surrender Value / cash-back rider. At the end of the CBO period, surrender value can equal **100%** (CBO 100) or **50%** (CBO 50) of base CBO premiums (not rider premiums), minus loans. Periods: **30 / 25 / 20** years.

Max issue: 30- and 25-year **55 NS / 50 nicotine**; 20-year **60 NS / 52 nicotine**. Min age 20.

Optional: ADB, additional insured, children’s term (up to $15,000/child), DI (also on AI), income term, involuntary unemployment WP, waiver of monthly specified premium.

### Term 125 and Term 100

Level SI term. Periods 30/25/20/15 with **full or 5-year guarantee**. Faces $25k–$450k.

Max ages: 30-yr **60**; 25-yr **65**; 20-yr **70**; 15-yr **75** (Term-at-a-glance). Min 20.

Term 125: built-in ADB **25%** of base + another **25%** common carrier. Term 100: **no** built-in ADB. Optional: additional insured, children’s term to $15k, DI, income term (125 only), IUWP, WP.

### Continuation 10 and Continuation 25

Term 125-style coverage that **continues permanently** after the level period at **10%** or **25%** of initial face. Premiums **never increase**. Faces $25k–$450k. Level periods 15/20/25/30. Max ages (glance): 15-yr 65, 20-yr 60, 25-yr 55, 30-yr 55.

### Payment Protector and Payment Protector Continuation

Decreasing term. Death benefit can pay as **level monthly income** until the end of the monthly-income period (**not less than 24 months**), or a **lump sum** (once monthly payments start, lump sum is gone). Min/max monthly income sized so initial lump is $25k–$450k.

Periods: 15/20/25/30 and **To Age 70**. Max ages: 15-yr **75**, 20 **70**, 25 **65**, 30 **60**, To-70 **50**.

ADB (optional extra $10k or $25k) if death from accident within 180 days + matching common carrier benefit.

**Payment Protector Continuation:** same, plus if the insured dies after the monthly-income period and monthly income was **not** used, a lump equal to **10%** of the initial lump sum. Permanent coverage; premiums never increase.

### ADB (accident-focused term)

$1,000 all-cause term DB plus ADB of **$100,000 / $150,000 / $200,000 / $250,000**. Common carrier benefit equals the ADB. 30-year level ages **20–50**; 20-year ages **51–60**. Same rates nicotine / non-nicotine. No living-benefit riders / no extra optional riders on the glance. Personal-history questions (not full medical). Position as least expensive / possible qualify with health conditions / supplement.

## Platinum Assure Series MYGA
