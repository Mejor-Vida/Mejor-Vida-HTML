## Hard stops

- **New York:** Americo not authorized. Never quote.
- **Foreign nationals** visiting / temporary US residence: not eligible (AdvantageWL / Term / IUL guides). Permanent residents with Green Card / I-551 and 1 continuous year in US may be considered.
- **Military bases:** agents may not sell Americo products on base.
- Rider holes (CI/chronic ADB, DI, unemployment waiver) exist on the national grid — always confirm the specific rider in the live eApp for that state.

## eApp vs paper

- Eagle Select, Instant Decision Term, Instant Decision IUL: eApp (`tools.americoagent.com`). Guide note: eApp not available in Cayman Islands.
- AdvantageWL: paper only.
