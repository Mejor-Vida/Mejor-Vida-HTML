**AAA8327:** applicant + agent both sign; **application and replacement notice must be signed on the same date**; leave a copy with the applicant. Financed purchases (borrowing/withdrawing from an existing policy to pay the new one) count as replacements. Agent must list company-approved sales-material form numbers used, or certify none were used, and leave copies with the applicant.

**ANV8327 (NV annuity):** includes a required written comparison of proposed vs existing annuity; NV free-look is **30 days** on the new contract.

Form 12-108-1: Replacement Appropriateness Agent Verification — complete when replacing existing life or annuity.

## AML / cash-value training

Agents who sell **cash-value** products (annuities, term with CV, whole life, UL) must complete LIMRA AML training **every two years**: `https://knowledge.limra.com` (username = NPN; first-time password = last name lowercase). LIMRA reports completion; no need to notify Americo. Help 866.364.2380.

ID for AML: full name, address history, DOB, place of birth, SSN/TIN, occupation, phone; review government photo ID. Red flags include product inconsistent with needs, reluctance to ID, unrelated third parties, focus on early-withdrawal penalties, large premium then uncooperative / not-taken.

## Annuity training

Product-specific training via LIMRA: `https://naic.pinpointglobal.com/Americo/apps/default.aspx` → My Product Training → Fixed Annuity or Indexed Annuity. Acknowledgement auto-sent to Americo within 48 hours. LIMRA tech 888.577.5522.
