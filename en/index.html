About final expense insurance

Also known as burial insurance or funeral insurance, final expense insurance is a permanent life policy with affordable premiums designed to cover funeral costs and related expenses when you die.

Upon your passing, the insurance company pays a cash benefit to your chosen beneficiaries, so your family does not have to face funeral costs, medical bills, and other end-of-life expenses alone.

Read the guide

Benefits

  • Coverage typically ranges from $2,000 to $50,000
  • Rates stay the same for life while premiums are paid on time
  • Loved ones can spend the money with no restrictions
  • Permanent coverage while the policy stays in force
  • Many simplified-issue plans: no medical exam
  • Simplified issue often has no waiting period
  • Options exist even with health conditions
  • Helps with funeral, medical debts, and related costs

Common questions

What is final expense insurance?

Final expense insurance is a type of life insurance specifically designed to cover costs associated with funeral, burial, medical bills, and other small end-of-life debts. It helps relieve the financial burden on your family during a difficult time.

How much does final expense insurance cost?

The cost varies based on your age, health, and the amount of coverage you choose. Premiums are typically affordable and remain level for the life of the policy. Request a free personalized quote online or via WhatsApp.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify?

In many cases, no medical exam is required. Many final expense policies use simplified health questionnaires. Depending on your answers and history, insurers may offer different levels of acceptance.

How long does it take to get a policy?

The process can be quick. After your quote form and a review with Julie (message or call once we have your details), many policies can be approved within days, depending on the insurer and your health.

Do you offer service in Spanish?

Yes, Julie offers personalized service in both Spanish and English. We understand the importance of communicating in your preferred language so you feel comfortable and fully understand your insurance options.
