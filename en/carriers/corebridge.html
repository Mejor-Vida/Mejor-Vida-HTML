Final expense and family protection life insurance

Corebridge Financial (policies issued by American General Life) offers final expense whole life solutions with simplified underwriting or guaranteed acceptance — designed to help cover funeral costs, small debts, and related end-of-life expenses.

Julie can compare SimpliNow Legacy® (level or graded death benefit) and GIWL (guaranteed issue), along with other carriers she offers, based on your age, health, state, and budget. She can also review term life and other permanent options when they fit.

Where Corebridge fits best

Corebridge final expense solutions are often a strong fit for:

  • Adults ages 50–80 seeking permanent coverage for funeral and final expenses
  • Applicants who can answer health questions and may qualify through a fast digital decision (SimpliNow Legacy)
  • People who prefer guaranteed acceptance with no health questions or exams (GIWL)
  • Families who want level premiums and flexible payment options (including Social Security debit / Direct Express® when available)
  • Clients in Nebraska, Kansas, Colorado, or Nevada whom Julie can quote as an independent agent

Final expense plan options

SimpliNow Legacy® Max (level benefit)

Best for: full face amount from day one when you qualify

  • Simplified-issue whole life; typical issue ages 50–80
  • Coverage from about $5,000; maximums vary by age (up to about $35,000 in some cases)
  • Death benefit equal to the face amount from the start (subject to policy terms)
  • Electronic application with a fast decision for many qualifying applicants
  • Level premiums; built-in limited-pay feature (premiums may stop based on policy design)

SimpliNow Legacy® (graded benefit)

Best for: when a graded design fits your health profile

  • Same SimpliNow Legacy line; typical issue ages 50–80
  • Typical amounts from $5,000 up to about $25,000
  • Years 1–2 (non-accidental death): generally a limited benefit based on premiums paid; then full face amount
  • Accidental death in the early years: typically pays face amount per the policy

GIWL — Guaranteed Issue Whole Life

Best for: guaranteed acceptance with no health questions

  • Typical ages 50–80; no health questions or medical exams
  • Coverage from about $5,000 to $25,000
  • Graded death benefit in the first two years; then full face amount
  • Fixed premiums; chronic or terminal illness accelerated benefits may be included at no extra premium charge where state-approved

Products, amounts, and riders are not available in all states. Julie confirms which plan applies in Nebraska, Kansas, Colorado, or Nevada.

Highlights (family-friendly summary)

  • Level premiums — designed not to increase because you age or your health changes after issue (when paid on time).
  • No medical exam on these final expense lines for qualifying applicants (or GIWL with no health questions).
  • Permanent whole life protection; cash values may apply per policy terms (loans reduce the benefit).
  • Flexible payments — typically card, bank draft, or Social Security debit / Direct Express® when available.
  • Other lines — Julie can also quote Select-a-Term and other Corebridge permanent options when they fit; she does not sell IUL or annuity products.

This is life insurance, not a prepaid funeral. The benefit can be used for any purpose the beneficiary chooses.
