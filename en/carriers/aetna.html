Final expense whole life insurance

Through Aetna Senior Supplemental, Julie can quote the portfolio’s two final expense whole life options: Accendo Final Expense (Accendo Insurance Company) and Protection Series℠ Final Expense (Continental Life / CLI) — both with no medical exam for applicants who qualify.

Availability depends on your state, eligibility, and plan. See the licenses page for current licensed states.

These are the only life insurance lines on this Aetna portal (no term, GUL, or IUL here). Medicare Supplement and indemnity/dental products are supplemental health, not life.

Where Aetna fits for final expense

Aetna’s FE whole life options often fit well for:

  • Families seeking permanent coverage for funeral and final expenses
  • Applicants who can qualify without a medical exam (health questions + database review)
  • Face amounts starting at $2,000 (lower maximums at older ages)
  • Comparing Accendo (from age 40; Level or Modified) vs Protection Series (from age 45; Level)
  • Clients in states where Mejor Vida is licensed

Life products (final expense)

Accendo — Level plan

Full benefit from day one · ages 40–89

  • Issuer: Accendo Insurance Company (Aetna / CVS Health affiliate)
  • Issue ages: 40–89 (age last birthday)
  • Coverage from $2,000; max by age — $50,000 (40–55), $40,000 (56–65), $30,000 (66–75), $25,000 (76–89)
  • Death benefit at face amount from issue (accident or natural causes)
  • Preferred / Standard / Super Preferred rates (Super Preferred ≈ 10% below Preferred when qualifies)
  • Annual policy fee: $40

Accendo — Modified plan

Graded-style design · ages 40–75

  • Same Accendo issuer
  • Issue ages: 40–75
  • Face amounts $2,000–$25,000
  • Accidental: full benefit immediately
  • Non-accidental: years 1–2 typically 110% of earned premiums; full from year 3
  • Level-plan riders not available · annual fee $40

Protection Series℠ Final Expense (CLI)

Level whole life · ages 45–89

  • Issuer: Continental Life Insurance Company of Brentwood, Tennessee (CLI)
  • Issue ages: 45–89
  • Level death benefits
  • Coverage $2,000–$50,000 (maximum varies by age at issue)
  • Riders: accidental death and children’s term (per product flyer)
  • Super Preferred available · billing can align with Social Security deposit date

Accendo riders (Level plan)

Accelerated death benefit

Pays up to 50% of the death benefit (subject to minimums/maximums and an administrative fee) if a physician certifies a terminal, chronic, or critical illness with life expectancy of 12 months or less. (Accendo Level.)

Accidental death benefit

Pays 100% of the base face amount for accidental death; typical Accendo issue ages 40–70. Also listed on Protection Series FE.

Children’s term insurance

Per-child coverage in increments (e.g. from $2,500 up to a per-child maximum), per rider rules — Accendo Level and Protection Series FE.
