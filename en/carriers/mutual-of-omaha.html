What is Whole Life Insurance?

Whole life insurance is permanent life insurance designed to stay in place for your entire life (as long as required premiums are paid). For many families, it is used to help cover final expenses and reduce financial stress on loved ones.

  • Lifetime coverage: Coverage does not expire after a set term.
  • Level premiums: Premium amounts are designed to stay predictable and not increase just because you age.
  • Guaranteed death benefit: Your beneficiary receives the policy benefit based on the plan terms.
  • Cash value growth: Many whole life policies build cash value over time that may be borrowed against.
  • Purpose: Helps families with funeral costs, debts, or other immediate expenses after a loss.

Plan Options

Level Benefit Plan

  • How it pays: Full death benefit from day 1 (if policy is in force).
  • Age range: 45-85.
  • Coverage amount: $2,000-$50,000 (WA: $5,000-$50,000).
  • Included rider: Accelerated Death Benefit for Terminal Illness or Nursing Home Confinement.
  • Optional rider: Accidental Death Benefit Rider.

Graded Benefit Plan*

  • How it pays: During years 1-2 for natural-cause death, benefit is premiums paid + 10%.
  • After year 2: Full death benefit is paid for all causes.
  • Accidental death: Full death benefit is paid in all years.
  • Age range: 45-80.
  • Coverage amount: $2,000-$20,000 (WA: $5,000-$20,000).

Level vs. Graded: Quick difference

  • Level is usually for people who can qualify for immediate full coverage.
  • Graded is often for applicants who need an easier approval path, with limited natural-cause payout in the first 2 years.

Options to Access Your Death Benefit Early

Accelerated Death Benefit for Terminal Illness or Nursing Home Confinement

Allows a one-time election if the insured is diagnosed with a terminal illness expected to result in death within 12 months, or has been confined to a nursing home for 90 consecutive days and is expected to remain confined for life.

Optional: Accidental Death Benefit Rider

Provides an additional death benefit equal to the policy face amount if death results from accidental bodily injury, independently of sickness and all other causes. Available for an additional premium.

**May not be available in all states.
