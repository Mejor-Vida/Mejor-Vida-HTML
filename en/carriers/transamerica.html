Final Expense Whole Life Insurance

Transamerica offers final expense whole life plans with simplified underwriting — no medical exam for many qualifying applicants — designed to help cover funeral costs, small debts, and related end-of-life expenses.

Mejor Vida Insurance can compare FE Express and the final expense portfolio (Immediate, 10-Pay, and Easy) alongside other carriers she offers, based on your age, health, state, and budget.

Where Transamerica Fits Best

Transamerica final expense solutions are often a strong fit for:

  • Families seeking permanent coverage and level premiums for funeral and final expenses
  • People who want a fast digital path (FE Express) with a quick decision when they qualify
  • Applicants who need a lower starting face amount (from $1,000 on Immediate / 10-Pay / Easy)
  • Those who may qualify for day-one full benefit or, based on health, a graded design (Graded / Easy)
  • Clients in states where Mejor Vida is licensed

Plan Options (Final Expense)

FE Express Solution℠

Best for: speed and full benefit from day one

  • Nonparticipating whole life; level premiums to age 121
  • Typical issue ages: 18–85
  • Coverage from $5,000 (Premier from $10,000); up to $100,000 (ages 18–75) or $25,000 (76–85)
  • Guaranteed face amount death benefit (subject to contestability/suicide periods and loans)
  • Digital process; no medical exam for many qualifying applicants

Graded FE Express Solution℠

Best for: when a graded benefit fits health underwriting

  • Typical issue ages: 18–80
  • Face amounts from $5,000 to $25,000
  • Years 1–2 (non-accidental death): generally 110% of premiums paid minus loans; then full face
  • Same Express line with level premiums to age 121

Immediate Solution

FE portfolio — day-one full benefit

  • Typical ages: 0–85
  • Minimum from $1,000; age-banded maximums (up to $50,000 at younger ages)
  • Premiums to age 121

10-Pay Solution

FE portfolio — pay for 10 years

  • Typical ages: 0–85
  • Full benefit from day one
  • Premiums scheduled for 10 years (then paid up)

Easy Solution

FE portfolio — graded design

  • Typical ages: 18–80
  • Up to $25,000
  • Years 1–2: limited benefit for non-accidental death; then full face

Products, amounts, and riders are not available in all states. FE Express / Graded FE Express are not available in New York. Julie confirms which plan applies in your state.

*Subject to contestability/suicide periods, loans, and policy terms. Approval depends on underwriting.

Highlights (family-friendly summary)

  • Level premiums — designed not to increase due to age or health changes after issue (when paid on time).
  • No medical exam for many qualifying applicants; eligibility depends on health questions and carrier rules.
  • Permanent whole life protection with possible cash value under policy terms (loans reduce the benefit).
  • Concierge Planning (where available on FE Express) — funeral guidance at no additional premium in approved states; not available everywhere.
  • Flexible payment options — typically monthly or annual; ACH, card, or Direct Express® depending on the product.

This is life insurance, not a prepaid funeral. Proceeds may be used for any purpose the beneficiary chooses.
