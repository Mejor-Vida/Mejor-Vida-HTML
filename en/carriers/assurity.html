Whole Life Insurance That Protects Your Loved Ones — For Life

Whole Life Protect+ and Whole Life Perform+ — Permanent coverage starting at $10,000 with built-in living benefits and no medical exam required for qualifying applicants.

When it comes to covering final expenses — funeral costs, outstanding bills, and debts you leave behind — a whole life policy from Assurity gives your family reliable, permanent protection. Coverage starts at $10,000, with simplified underwriting for applicants through age 85, so many people can get covered without a medical exam.

Note: Assurity does not sell a product branded “final expense.” Whole Life Protect+ and Whole Life Perform+ are often used for final expense planning because minimum face amounts start at $10,000 and underwriting is simplified.

Where Assurity Fits Best

Assurity whole life is a great fit for:

  • Seniors seeking affordable, permanent coverage for final expenses
  • Parents and grandparents helping children or grandchildren build a strong financial start
  • Clients who want permanent protection with built-in living benefits
  • People who value lifelong coverage that builds cash value
  • Anyone who wants premiums locked in and coverage that cannot be cancelled as long as premiums are paid

Plan Options

Whole Life Protect+

Best for: Maximizing the death benefit

  • Designed to maximize the death benefit for the premium paid
  • Strong choice when the priority is leaving money for funeral and final expenses
  • Competitive pricing for seniors on a fixed income
  • Built-in Accelerated Death Benefit Rider included at no extra cost
  • Face amounts: $10,000 and above · Issue ages: 15 days through 85 years

Whole Life Perform+

Best for: Building cash value

  • Designed for stronger cash value accumulation over time
  • Ideal if you want to build a financial asset alongside your coverage
  • Cash value can be accessed for goals like college, caregiving, or emergencies
  • Eligible for dividends — Assurity has a long history of dividend payments
  • Face amounts: $10,000 and above · Issue ages: 15 days through 85 years

Built-In Living Benefits

Form No. R I2422 — Living Benefits Rider. Included automatically at no additional cost.

Living Benefits — Included at No Extra Cost

Every Assurity whole life policy includes the Accelerated Death Benefit Rider automatically at no additional cost — so your policy can help during life, not only after.

Terminal Illness Benefit

If diagnosed with a terminal illness with life expectancy of 12 months or less (certified by a physician), you may access part of your death benefit early.

  • Maximum acceleration: up to $1,000,000 combined
  • At least $10,000 must remain in the policy

Chronic Illness Benefit

If certified unable to perform at least two Activities of Daily Living without substantial assistance for 90 consecutive days, you may access part of your benefit. Available in any 12-month period up to IRS per diem limits; the condition does not need to be permanent.

Critical Illness Benefit

If diagnosed with or undergoing a covered critical illness or procedure, you may access part of your benefit. Covered conditions include:

  • Heart attack (myocardial infarction)
  • Stroke
  • Invasive cancer
  • Coronary artery bypass surgery
  • Sudden cardiac arrest
  • Kidney (renal) failure
  • Major organ transplant
  • Traumatic brain injury
  • Aortic aneurysm
  • Severe burns
  • Paralysis
  • Coma
  • Motor neuron disease
  • Loss of sight
  • And more — see policy materials for full definitions

Chronic and critical illness options available to applicants through issue age 70.
