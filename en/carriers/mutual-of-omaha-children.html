Children's Whole Life insurance

Mutual of Omaha Children's Whole Life (CWL) is a standalone simplified-issue whole life policy for children. Mejor Vida Insurance can help families compare this path with other appointed carriers.

Children's life insurance cost · Mutual of Omaha carrier overview

At a glance

In plain terms: this is lifelong coverage for a child. Premiums stay level, a cash value can grow over time, and the policy is designed to leave a set benefit for the family when it’s needed most.

Who can be covered & for how much

  • Available for children from 14 days old through age 17
  • Coverage (the amount paid to beneficiaries) can be about $5,000 to $50,000
  • Simple health review only — no doctor visit or medical exam
  • One standard rate class (no higher “table” rate categories on this product)

How you pay

  • You can pay yearly, twice a year, quarterly, or monthly (monthly is usually by bank draft)
  • There is a small extra policy fee of about $12 per year
  • As long as premiums are paid as required, your price does not go up and the coverage amount does not go down

Cash value & payout

  • The policy can build cash value over time that the owner may borrow against (loan interest is about 5.66%)
  • You generally cannot take partial cash withdrawals — access is through a policy loan instead
  • If premiums stay current, the death benefit stays in force through age 100

Optional add-ons (riders)

Riders are optional extras you can add to the policy. On Children’s Whole Life, two common ones help families keep coverage flexible as the child grows — and help keep the policy paid if the owner is no longer here.

Guaranteed Insurability

  • Lets the family buy more coverage later without a new health review (no new medical exam for eligible options)
  • Age windows after the child turns 25, 30, 35, and 40
  • Also available around big life moments: marriage/civil union, welcoming a child, or buying a home
  • Up to 5 times total; each option usually must be used within 6 months
  • Mutual of Omaha typically sends a reminder about 60 days before age-based windows

Premium help if the owner passes away

  • If the policy owner dies, premiums can be paused for a one-time period of 90 days
  • This help becomes available 24 months after the policy starts
  • Those paused premiums are not taken out of the policy’s benefit amount

How the application works

  • You can apply for up to 8 children on one form — each child still gets their own policy
  • Grandparents may be able to sign without a parent’s signature (when product rules allow)
  • This product does not use a “conditional receipt” (temporary coverage paperwork some other policies use)

Other Mutual of Omaha options for kids

Besides the standalone Children’s Whole Life policy, Mutual of Omaha also has other ways to cover a child. These are different products — Mejor Vida Insurance can help you compare which path fits your family.

Larger coverage with a full health review

  • Usually for children from about 15 days through age 17
  • Higher coverage amounts are possible — often up to about $250,000 (larger amounts need more review)
  • A child’s coverage is usually limited to about half of what the parent with less life insurance already has
  • This path is not available in Washington

Add-on coverage on a parent’s policy

  • Instead of a separate kids policy, some adult policies can add a small child rider
  • Combined rider coverage is often limited to about $10,000 per child
  • Ask Mejor Vida Insurance for a personalized illustration — rider pricing depends on the adult policy
