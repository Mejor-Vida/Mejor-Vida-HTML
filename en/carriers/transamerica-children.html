Immediate Solution for kids & child/grandchild add-on

Transamerica Immediate Solution is a permanent life insurance plan that can cover children as well as adults. On some adult Immediate Solution policies, families can also add a small child or grandchild add-on. Mejor Vida Insurance can help compare which path fits.

Children's life insurance guide · Children's life insurance cost · Transamerica carrier overview

At a glance — Immediate Solution

In plain terms: Immediate Solution is lifelong coverage with a set benefit. When the policy is issued as “Immediate,” the full benefit amount is designed to be in force from day one (as long as the policy stays in force under its terms).

Who can be covered & for how much

  • For children, coverage usually starts around $1,000 and can go up to about $50,000
  • Immediate Solution is the same product adults can buy — so the carrier’s overall age range is 0–85, not just kids
  • At older adult ages the company allows a smaller maximum (about $40,000 at 56–65, $30,000 at 66–75, and $25,000 at 76–85). Those adult caps don’t change the children’s limit above
  • Children may qualify under preferred or standard kids rate classes (ask for an illustration)

Ownership & eligibility notes

  • A parent or grandparent can own the policy even when the child is the person covered
  • If health questions show “yes” in two or more medical categories, this path is usually not available
  • A history of childhood cancer typically means this product is not offered — Mejor Vida Insurance can help look at other options

What families often like

  • Full benefit from day one when issued as Immediate Solution
  • Can start coverage when a child is very young
  • Optional add-on for children or grandchildren on some adult policies (details below)

Optional child/grandchild add-on (on an adult’s policy)

This add-on (sometimes called the Children’s/Grandchildren’s Benefit Rider) attaches to Immediate Solution only — not to Transamerica’s other final-expense designs on this page. Exact pricing should still be confirmed on an illustration.

Who it covers & what it costs

  • Adds temporary coverage for children or grandchildren named on the rider
  • Premium is very low because the add-on amounts are small: about $2 per year for each $1,000 of coverage, per child (example: about $10 a year for $5,000 on one child)
  • Parent or grandparent owner ages 18–75; child ages 15 days through 18
  • Up to 9 children or grandchildren on one rider

How much coverage

  • Usually from $1,000 up to the lesser of the adult policy’s coverage or $5,000 per child
  • Every child on the rider gets the same coverage amount
  • Across Transamerica final-expense child/grandchild add-ons, total coverage is generally capped at about $5,000 per child

When it ends & converting later

  • Coverage for each child typically ends on the rider anniversary after they turn 25
  • After 2 years on the rider, the family may convert to a permanent policy without a new health review (kids’ classes apply by age)
  • If a child already has a terminal illness expected within 24 months, they usually cannot be added to this rider

Two common ways families use Transamerica for kids

These are different paths. Mejor Vida Insurance can help you compare a policy in the child’s name versus a small temporary add-on on a parent’s or grandparent’s plan.

A permanent policy for the child

  • The child is the insured on Immediate Solution
  • Coverage amounts follow the age-based limits above
  • A parent or grandparent can own the policy while the child is young

A temporary add-on on an adult’s policy

  • An adult buys Immediate Solution and adds the child/grandchild rider
  • Usually smaller amounts (often up to about $5,000 per child) that end around age 25
  • Often used when the goal is modest short-term protection for several children or grandchildren
