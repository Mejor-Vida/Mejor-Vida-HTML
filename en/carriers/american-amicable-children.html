Children’s & grandchild coverage options

American Amicable can cover kids in a few different ways: a small add-on on some adult final-expense policies, or a Family Solution / Family Choice plan that can start at younger ages (including children). Mejor Vida Insurance can help compare — exact add-on dollar amounts need an illustration.

Children's life insurance guide · Children's life insurance cost · American Amicable carrier overview

At a glance

In plain terms: American Amicable is often used for adult final-expense coverage, and some of those adult plans can add a little protection for children or grandchildren. Separately, Family Solution / Family Choice can insure younger people — including kids — on their own policy.

Add-on on an adult’s policy

  • Some adult plans can add a small amount of coverage for grandchildren or children
  • These are usually modest, temporary extras — not a full children’s whole-life policy by themselves
  • Exact coverage amounts and premiums: ask for an illustration

A policy for younger ages (including kids)

  • Family Solution and Family Choice are whole-life style plans for ages 0–49
  • Coverage usually starts around $10,000
  • For many “immediate” benefit designs, the maximum is about $35,000 (ages 0–49)

What to confirm with us

  • Which path fits — add-on vs. a policy in the child’s name
  • Current availability in your state
  • A personalized illustration before applying

Optional add-ons on adult final-expense plans

These extras attach to an adult’s policy. Names like “GCIA” or “CIA” are carrier form labels — what matters for families is who gets covered and that dollar amounts must be confirmed on an illustration.

Grandchild add-on

  • Available on adult plans such as Golden Solution and Senior Choice (typically ages 50–85 for the adult)
  • Adds a small amount of coverage for grandchildren
  • Spanish materials are available for some forms
  • Coverage amount and price: ask Mejor Vida Insurance for an illustration

Children’s add-on

  • Available on some final-expense products as a children’s insurance agreement
  • Puts coverage on children named under the adult’s policy
  • Exact coverage and premium: ask for an illustration

Family Solution / Family Choice (younger ages)

These plans are designed for people under 50 — including children — rather than only seniors. They are a different path from adding a grandchild rider onto a senior final-expense policy.

Who can be covered & for how much

  • Issue ages: 0 through 49
  • Minimum coverage typically about $10,000
  • On common immediate-benefit designs: maximum about $35,000 for ages 0–49
  • A return-of-premium style option (ages 18–49) usually tops out around $20,000

How families often use this

  • A permanent-style policy for a child or younger adult under one of these family plans
  • Useful when you want coverage in the child’s name, not only a small add-on on a grandparent’s policy
  • Mejor Vida Insurance can compare this with Mutual of Omaha, Assurity, Transamerica, and other appointed options

Rules when applying for a child

American Amicable has some common-sense guidelines when the person covered is a child. These help keep coverage amounts realistic for the family.

  • Children ages 0–17 in the same family are generally expected to have similar coverage amounts when applicable
  • A child’s coverage usually should not be higher than what the parents (or legal guardians) already have
  • Parents or legal guardians typically must already have life insurance in force when applying for the child
  • If a grandparent or guardian applies, guardianship papers may be required
