Weekly Insurance Industry Update

February 16, 2026 – February 22, 2026 | Julie, Licensed Insurance Agent | 8 min read

Final expense and life insurance market intelligence for consumers and licensed agents.

Author: Mejor Vida Insurance Editorial Team

Reviewed by: Julie Braunsroth, Licensed Life & Health Insurance Agent

Quick Answer

Life insurance demand remains strong in early 2026, while regulation and training standards continue to tighten. Key headlines include a major jump in application volume, Oklahoma's adoption of the NAIC 2025 Valuation Manual, a new structured training model for agents, and a large policy transfer from failed carriers to Continental General that shows how guaranty systems protect policyholders.

Key Takeaways

  • Term applications rose 26.4%, whole life 15.6%, and universal life 34.7% year-over-year.
  • Oklahoma adopted the NAIC 2025 Valuation Manual effective January 1, 2026.
  • A structured training model may improve agent consistency and compliance outcomes.
  • Continental General acquired around 91,000 policies from guaranty associations.
  • UK regulators are exploring alternative capital tools that may influence global markets.

Definitions

  • Valuation Manual: Standards insurers use to measure policy liabilities and reserves.
  • Guaranty association: State-level safety net that helps protect policyholders when carriers fail.
  • Alternative capital: Capital sources beyond traditional equity and debt, such as ILS structures.

1. U.S. Life Insurance Applications Surge Across Product Types

Source: MIB Group / LifeHealth | Published: February 10, 2026

January data shows broad-based momentum in life insurance demand. Growth in term, whole life, and universal life suggests that more families are prioritizing protection and long-term planning.

Why This Matters

Higher application volume usually means stronger consumer awareness and more opportunities to compare plans before rates or eligibility conditions change.

2. Oklahoma Adopts the NAIC 2025 Valuation Manual

This update strengthens reserve and valuation standards used by life and health carriers. Stronger valuation rules can improve long-term insurer stability.

3. New Structured Agent Development Model Launches

The framework emphasizes licensing, field training, documentation, recurring evaluations, and mentorship to reduce performance variability and compliance risk.

4. Continental General Acquires 91,000 Policies

The transfer from guaranty associations after carrier liquidations highlights how policyholder protection mechanisms work in real-world scenarios.

5. UK Regulators Explore Alternative Capital

The PRA review of ILS and related structures could influence how insurers globally think about risk transfer and capital efficiency over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much did life insurance applications increase in January 2026?
Reported growth was 26.4% for term life, 15.6% for whole life, and 34.7% for universal life year-over-year.
Why does the NAIC Valuation Manual matter to families?
It helps define reserve and liability standards used by insurers, which supports long-term claims-paying stability.
What does the 91,000-policy transfer show?
It demonstrates that guaranty systems can help preserve policyholder coverage continuity after insurer failures.

Conclusion

The February 16 – February 22, 2026 update points to a market with strong demand, tighter standards, and active policyholder protections. For consumers, this is a good time to review coverage and carrier quality with a licensed agent.
