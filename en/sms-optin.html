SMS Opt-In Disclosure

Mejor Vida Insurance LLC sends SMS text messages to consumers who provide express written consent on our website. This page describes our SMS program, how you opt in, and your rights. Nebraska Department of Insurance producer license #21695431.

Program Description

Our SMS program provides informational messages about final expense and life insurance options, including personalized quotes, appointment reminders, application follow-up, and policy-related updates. We also send one-time verification codes by SMS when you request phone verification on our quote forms. Messages are sent from our SMS number 402-844-1199.

Phone Verification (2FA)

On our quote landing page, you may enter your mobile number and click Send verification code to receive a one-time SMS with a 6-digit code. By clicking that button, you agree to receive that verification message. Verification codes expire in 10 minutes. This is separate from optional marketing/follow-up SMS consent below.

How You Opt In

You may opt in by providing your mobile phone number and optionally checking the SMS consent box on one of our online quote forms before you submit. The checkbox is not required to submit the form or receive a quote. Express written SMS consent is recorded only when you check the box and submit. Consent is not a condition of purchasing insurance where prohibited by law.
