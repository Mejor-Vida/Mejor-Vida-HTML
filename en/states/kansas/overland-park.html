What final expense insurance is

It is whole life insurance in a modest amount ($5,000 to $25,000 is typical). The benefit is paid in cash to your beneficiary. It can cover a funeral, cremation, medical bills, and small debts. It is not a prepaid funeral: it is not tied to one funeral home or one plot.

It is also called burial insurance or funeral insurance. That is the same smaller permanent life product; the name does not require spending the check on a specific funeral. What this insurance is.

  • Simplified issue: health questions, no exam. If approved, the full benefit usually pays from day one.
  • Guaranteed acceptance: no health questions, with a two- or three-year waiting period.
  • Level premiums when paid on time; the policy does not expire at a set age the way term does.

The burial plot is a separate bill

The prices in the table above are from the funeral home. They do not include a burial plot (the grave space in the ground).

If there is a burial, the cemetery sends its own bill. That bill is usually:

  • the grave space (the plot)
  • opening and closing the grave
  • almost always a vault and a grave marker

Those items are not in the funeral packages.

Buying a resale plot (usually cheaper)

If someone already owns a plot and will not use it, they can sell it. Private asking prices are usually lower than the cemetery’s list. The cemetery still charges a transfer fee (ads cite $295–$495) and must put the deed in the buyer’s name.

Overland Park FAQs

Is it the same as burial or funeral insurance?

Yes. Final expense, burial, and funeral insurance usually describe the same smaller permanent life policy. In Overland Park the check is paid in cash to your beneficiary; it is not tied to one funeral home or one plot.

Do you serve Leawood and other towns near Overland Park?

Yes. Official quotes are by phone for Kansas residents in Overland Park, Leawood, Prairie Village, Mission, and Fairway. There is no public walk-in office.

Do I need a medical exam?

Many final expense policies in Kansas use health questions and no exam. If health is a barrier, guaranteed acceptance may be available, usually with a waiting period.

Does the policy pay from day one?

If you are approved for simplified issue, the full benefit typically pays from day one on covered claims. Guaranteed issue pays a limited benefit for the first two or three years, then the full benefit.

How much coverage is usually enough for cremation in Overland Park?

The FCA-GKC 2025 survey has no cremation-with-memorial package, so this table does not name funeral homes. The estimator uses $2,553 as the Kansas average for direct cremation; that figure is not one home’s price. Many families choose $5,000 to $10,000 for the service, urn, travel, and small bills. A traditional burial often needs more: the Kansas average is $8,640, and the plot, vault, and marker are extra.

What is the difference between a prepaid funeral and this insurance?

A prepaid plan is tied to one funeral home or plot and may lock that price. Final expense insurance pays cash to your beneficiary. They can use it at any funeral home in Overland Park, Johnson County Memorial Gardens, or for other final bills.

Does the funeral include the cemetery plot?

No. The funeral home price is one bill. The plot is another. Ask Johnson County Memorial Gardens (913-451-1860) for its list. Resale plots can cost less; the cemetery still has to change the deed.
