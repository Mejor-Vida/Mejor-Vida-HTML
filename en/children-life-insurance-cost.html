What is whole life insurance for children?

Whole life for a child is permanent coverage that stays in force for life if premiums are paid. Premiums are usually level from issue. Many plans also build cash value over time that the owner may borrow against per the contract.

Families often use it to lock in insurability early, help with future final expenses, or gift a small permanent policy. Sibling amounts are often kept similar; many carriers expect a parent or guardian to have adequate coverage in force.

Participating (dividend) whole life

Some permanent policies may pay dividends when declared — not guaranteed. Dividends can buy paid-up additions, reduce premiums, or be taken in cash, depending on the contract. This page does not chart dividend illustrations.

Limited-pay (10-pay / 20-pay)

Limited-pay whole life concentrates premiums into fewer years (for example 10 or 20), then the policy can be paid-up while coverage continues. Ask Mejor Vida Insurance whether a limited-pay design fits your family.

Adult whole life cost page · Ask about limited-pay for a child

What is universal life insurance?

Universal life (UL) is permanent life insurance with flexible premiums and a cash-value account that earns interest (or indexed/variable crediting, depending on the product). Unlike traditional whole life, UL premiums and the death benefit can often be adjusted within policy limits — but if the policy is underfunded, it can lapse.

For children, simplified whole life is more common than UL. This page explains UL for education only and does not show UL premium charts.

Talk with Mejor Vida Insurance before funding a UL design

Higher coverage amounts for children

Simplified kids whole life is often capped around $50,000. Larger amounts may need different products and underwriting. Contact Mejor Vida Insurance if you need more coverage.

Children’s coverage by appointed carrier

These appointed carriers offer clear coverage for kids — either a policy of their own or an add-on on an adult’s policy:

Children’s Whole Life — a permanent policy just for the child (about $5,000–$50,000), from 14 days through age 17. Simple application, no medical exam.

Protect+ / Perform+ — permanent life insurance that can start in early childhood and continue into adulthood (same product family).

Immediate Solution — permanent coverage for children ages 0–17, with a simple application. Some adult plans can also add a child or grandchild add-on.

Some adult final-expense plans can add a small amount of coverage for grandchildren.

Some final-expense plans allow a temporary child or grandchild add-on (small amounts per child).

Some adult term or permanent plans can include a temporary child add-on.

AdvantageWL can insure a child from age 0 (about $15,000 minimum). Eagle Select also offers a child/grandchild term rider on an adult policy.

What drives the cost of children’s life insurance?

Age at issue

Younger issue ages usually mean lower premiums. Waiting a few years can raise the price.

Gender

In most states, girls often cost slightly less than boys at the same age and class.

Face amount

More coverage means a higher premium. Cost does not always rise in a straight line with the face amount.

Product design

SI whole life, limited-pay, riders, or UL funding all change cost. Charts above are SI whole-life samples only.

Health & underwriting

Even for kids, health history and build guidelines matter. Simplified issue still asks questions.

State & carrier

Availability and forms follow the child’s / owner’s state and the carrier’s rules.

Frequently asked questions

How much does children’s life insurance cost per month?

On these sample charts, about $8–$19/month for $10,000 and roughly $21–$81/month for $50,000 depending on age and gender. Your quote may differ.

Is whole life or term better for a child?

Many families choose small permanent (whole life) policies for kids. Child term riders on an adult policy are another path. Mejor Vida Insurance can help match the product to your goal.

What is universal life in plain language?

Permanent coverage with flexible premiums and a cash-value account. Premiums and death benefit can often be adjusted within limits; underfunding can cause a lapse. Whole life is more common for children through Mejor Vida Insurance.

Can grandparents buy coverage on a grandchild?

Often yes, subject to carrier rules. Some adult policies also offer a grandchild rider. See the life insurance for grandchildren guide.

Do these charts guarantee my price?

No. They are educational samples only. Mejor Vida Insurance will quote current options for your child’s age, health, and coverage amount.
