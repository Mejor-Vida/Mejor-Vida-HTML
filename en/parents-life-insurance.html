Life insurance for a parent: you can pay; they still have to take part

Most families do this for a funeral, not to replace a paycheck. That matches the insurable interest the NAIC describes. What you cannot skip: the parent consents, answers health questions if the plan has them, and signs. You can ask for quotes first. The application is with them.

Requirements Owner Type Cost Signing Questions

Three facts to start with

  • You can talk with Mejor Vida Insurance and see premiums without your parents on the call. To issue the policy, they are required.
  • If they qualify for a level plan (health questions, no in-office exam), the full benefit can apply from the first payment. Guaranteed acceptance skips the questions and adds about a two-year wait.
  • You should be the owner if you pay the premium. If the parent is listed as owner, the company will not discuss the contract with you.

Use your parent’s age and tobacco, not yours. Appointed-company figures. Not an offer.

What has to be true on application day

These conditions are not “negotiated” with an agent. If one is missing, there is no new policy.

  • They are in the United States when you apply and have an SSN or TIN.
  • They have the mental capacity to sign a contract. Advanced dementia usually closes plans that ask health questions.
  • They agree that you are buying coverage on their life and they sign (voice, electronic, or paper).
  • If you want a plan without a two-year wait, they answer the health questions — not you on their behalf.

Why owner matters more than payer

The application can name an owner other than the insured. At Mutual of Omaha, an adult child is on the accepted-owner list. That lets you change beneficiaries and request information. It also keeps cash value in your estate, not your parent’s — a point that sometimes comes up in a Medicaid review. That is not legal advice.

Online forms from some advertised brands usually leave the insured as owner. You would pay and could not cancel or ask questions. Mejor Vida Insurance sets the owner on the appointed-company application.

Which type to ask for on a parent

The usual goal is cash for a funeral. NFDA’s 2023 study reported U.S. medians of $8,300 with viewing and burial and $6,280 with viewing and cremation; the cemetery is extra. A $10,000–$25,000 final expense policy covers that range for many families.

  1. Level final expense. The usual path. Living Promise ages 45–85, up to about $50,000. Accendo Level through 89 ($25,000 at 76–89). Transamerica Immediate Solution through 85. Health questions; no lab appointment on these plans.
  2. Guaranteed acceptance. If the questionnaire does not produce a level plan. Appointed GIWL: generally 50–80, $5,000–$25,000, two-year wait for natural death. Guaranteed acceptance guide.
  3. Term. Only if a mortgage or a set income period remains. By 75–80 a long term is almost gone. Term life.

Health: do not assume they “cannot qualify”

Controlled diabetes, high blood pressure, or an old heart attack do not automatically close a level plan. What often pushes guaranteed issue: dementia, dialysis, home oxygen, an active cancer, or being in a facility. Each company has its list. Mejor Vida Insurance compares before you apply blind.

How you sign with a parent

  1. Voice. The agent reads the questions. Parent and child say their name and the date. This works when you live in different states.
  2. Link. An email or text arrives. Each person signs on screen.
  3. Paper. Uncommon. Both sign the same set. Do not sign “for” them.

Frequently asked questions

Can I insure both parents on one policy?

No. Each insured life needs its own contract. You can own both and pay both premiums.

What if my parent will not talk about dying?

Start with the number: “this would cost X a month for Y of benefit.” If they will not sign, there is no policy. Saving or a prepaid funeral are the alternatives, not an insurance shortcut.

Does the money have to go to the funeral home?

No. The beneficiary receives cash and decides. Name a specific person and tell them.

Through age 90?

We do not publish new final-expense issue at 90. Accendo Level can go through 89. See the seniors guide.

Can I quote by myself?

Yes, the first conversation. Bring age, tobacco, and a health summary. They join when there is an application.
