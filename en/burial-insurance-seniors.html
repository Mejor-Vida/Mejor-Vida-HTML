Burial insurance for seniors: a clear guide

Burial insurance — also called final expense insurance — is whole life meant for a funeral, cremation, and small debts. Mejor Vida Insurance quotes appointed companies. Simplified-issue plans skip the in-office exam; they use health questions.

How it works What it covers Cost Apply Companies FAQ

Key points

  • It is whole life: it lasts as long as premiums are paid. The beneficiary receives cash and can use it for the funeral or other bills.
  • Many appointed companies issue through age 85. Accendo Level can go through age 89, with a $25,000 cap at later ages.
  • A level or immediate plan can pay the full amount from day one if you qualify. Guaranteed acceptance usually has a two-year wait.

“Burial insurance,” “funeral insurance,” and “final expense” are usually the same kind of product: a smaller whole life policy with simplified underwriting.

How it works

You choose an amount, answer health questions, and the insurer reviews your answers and databases. It may offer an immediate (level) plan, a graded plan, or only guaranteed acceptance. It is not automatic approval.

Two usual paths

Simplified issue (the usual path)

  • Typical ages: 45 or 50 through 85; Accendo Level through 89
  • Typical amounts: $2,000–$50,000 ($25,000 cap at later ages)

There is a questionnaire. There is no lab appointment. Mutual of Omaha Living Promise Level issues ages 45–85. Transamerica Immediate Solution goes through 85. See no medical exam.

Guaranteed acceptance (Plan B)

  • Ages: each product sets its range
  • Amounts: often top out near $25,000

Few or no health questions. In return there is almost always a two-year wait for natural death. It helps if you cannot qualify for a level plan. It is not the first path if you can still answer a questionnaire.

What burial insurance covers

The policy pays a set amount to the beneficiary. That money is often used for:

  • Funeral services and funeral-home fees
  • Burial or cremation
  • A headstone or other memorial
  • Medical bills, debts, or family travel

The contract does not pay the funeral home by itself: the beneficiary receives the benefit and decides how to use it. A common amount for a simple funeral is about $10,000 to $25,000; Mejor Vida Insurance confirms the amount for your case.

How much burial insurance costs

Price rises with age, sex, tobacco, and health. On appointed companies, a level $10,000 policy at age 50 is often near $28 a month for a woman and $34 a month for a man (non-tobacco, good health). By age 65 those figures are near $41 and $54. They are educational premiums from the Mejor Vida Insurance quoter (August 2026), not an offer.

Is there a waiting period?

Not always. If you qualify for a level or immediate plan, year 1 can show the full amount for natural death. Guaranteed acceptance and many graded plans do have a two-year wait. Ask for the net death-benefit table. More detail on no-waiting-period coverage.

Is it a fit for you?

It often fits if you want to leave money for a funeral and do not have enough savings or a permanent policy already in force. It may not be needed if you already prepaid a funeral, set cash aside, or own a permanent policy of about the same size.

After 80 and after 85

At 80, appointed final expense options still exist. After 85 there are fewer companies and lower amounts; Accendo Level can issue through 89. See seniors over 80 and seniors over 85.

How to apply for a senior final expense policy

The process is short. It is not a funeral-home contract: it is a whole life application with an appointed company. Mejor Vida Insurance compares options and explains the result before you sign.

  1. Ask for a quote with age, tobacco, state, and the amount you have in mind. Start with a free quote or call 402-440-5438.
  2. Answer the health questions honestly. A missed “yes” can delay or void a claim.
  3. The insurer reviews your answers and databases. It may offer a level plan, a graded plan, or only guaranteed acceptance.
  4. You (or the policy owner) review the benefit table, the premium, and any wait. If it fits, you sign and pay the first premium.

Keep this in mind

Most of these policies are sold through a licensed agent, not a TV ad. An independent agent can compare appointed companies and tell you whether there is a wait or day-one benefit.

Buying burial insurance for a parent

Adult children often help compare prices. That is fine. The person whose life is insured almost always has to take part: sign, answer health questions, and consent. You can be the owner or the payer; the parent is the insured.

  • Do not hide medical history. The company checks it. Incomplete answers can affect the payout.
  • Name clear beneficiaries and tell them where the policy is. The money goes to the beneficiary, not the funeral home.
  • If the parent is over 80, start with a level plan if they can still answer questions. See seniors over 80.

Frequently asked questions

Is burial insurance the same as final expense?

In practice, yes: it is a smaller whole life policy for a funeral and small debts. Mejor Vida Insurance quotes it as final expense.

Do I need a medical exam?

On the simplified plans we quote, no. There are health questions. See no medical exam.

Until what age can I buy it?

It depends on the product. Many appointed companies issue through 85; Accendo Level can go through 89. See the age limit guide.

Can the money only be used for a funeral?

No. The beneficiary receives a cash payment. They can use it for the funeral, debts, or other expenses.

What does it cost at 70 or 80?

It rises with age. Use the table on this page (illustrative appointed-company premiums) and ask for a quote for your age, tobacco, and state.

If I already have savings or a prepaid funeral, do I still need this?

Not always. If those funds cover the cost, a new policy may not be needed. Mejor Vida Insurance can help compare the remaining gap.

Who qualifies for burial insurance?

Someone in the product’s issue-age range, in a state Mejor Vida Insurance can quote, who passes that plan’s underwriting. Health decides whether you get level, graded, or only guaranteed acceptance. Licensed states are on the licenses page.

Can seniors over 80 get final expense insurance?

Yes. Appointed companies still have options at 80. After 85 there are fewer companies and lower amounts; Accendo Level can issue through 89. See seniors over 80.

Is term life a good way to cover funeral costs?

It is usually a poor fit. Term ends; a funeral does not. If the term runs out at 70 or 75, you can be left without coverage just when a burial amount is most useful. Final expense is smaller whole life.

How much coverage should I buy?

A simple funeral is often about $10,000 to $25,000. Add small debts or family travel if you want a cushion. The table on this page shows illustrative premiums; Mejor Vida Insurance confirms the amount in a quote.

How do you file a claim?

The beneficiary contacts the insurer (or Mejor Vida Insurance) with the death certificate and policy number. Payment goes to the named beneficiary, not automatically to the funeral home. Keep the policy and the agent’s number where the family can find them.

Will beneficiaries owe tax on the money?

In most cases the death benefit is not taxable income to the beneficiary. This is not tax advice: a CPA or attorney should review large estates or other assets. The money usually skips probate if the beneficiary is named correctly.

What is cash value?

It is a small savings amount some whole life policies build over time. On final expense the main job is the death benefit, not investing. A loan or a surrender can reduce what the family receives.

Is burial insurance the same as funeral insurance?

In practice, yes: “burial,” “funeral,” and “final expense” describe the same class of smaller whole life. The contract does not reserve a service at a specific funeral home; it pays cash to the beneficiary.
