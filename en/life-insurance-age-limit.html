What is the age limit for buying life insurance?

There is no universal maximum age. Each product and each insurer sets the last age it will take a new application. For final expense, many Mejor Vida Insurance appointed companies issue through 85; Aetna Accendo can go through 89.

No single cap Overview Final expense Cost FAQ

Key points

  • The limit depends on the policy type and the company — not one federal rule.
  • Term usually cuts off earlier. A 30-year term is not available at age 80.
  • For final expense, many appointed companies take new applications through age 85. Accendo Level can issue through age 89, with a $25,000 cap at later ages.

Issue age means “will they take a new application today?” A whole life policy you already own does not cancel just because you have a birthday, if premiums stay paid.

There is no single “age cap”

The law does not say “nobody can buy life insurance after age X.” What matters is each product’s issue age: the last age the insurer will accept a new application. After that, there is no new policy with that company and product — even if you already own a policy that stays in force.

Final expense insurance age limit

  • New-applicant age: often through 85; Accendo Level through 89
  • How long it lasts: for life, if premiums are paid

Final expense is whole life meant for a funeral, cremation, and small debts. Mejor Vida Insurance quotes simplified plans (health questions, no in-office exam). Mutual of Omaha Living Promise Level issues ages 45–85. Accendo Level can issue later, with a $25,000 cap at ages 76–89. See final expense insurance and no medical exam.

Guaranteed acceptance age limit

  • New-applicant age: each product sets its own range
  • How long it lasts: for life if premiums are paid, with a typical two-year wait for natural death

There are few or no health questions. In return there is almost always a two-year wait for natural death. It is a Plan B if you cannot qualify for a level plan. It is not the first path if you can still answer a questionnaire.

Term life insurance age limit

  • New-applicant age: depends on the term; a 30-year term is not available at 80
  • How long it lasts: until the term ends, not “forever”

Term covers a set number of years. Some appointed products accelerate underwriting and skip the lab if you qualify. It is not the typical burial product after 70. Confirm term length, tobacco, and age on the term life quote.

Whole life insurance age limit

  • New-applicant age: for final expense, usually the same 85 band (or 89 on Accendo)
  • How long it lasts: as long as premiums are paid

Final expense is whole life. Larger whole life amounts sometimes need more underwriting and cut off earlier. To cover a funeral, the product we quote is simplified final expense, not a six-figure whole life policy.

How price changes with age

On appointed companies, a level $10,000 policy at age 65 is often near $41 a month for a woman and $54 a month for a man (non-tobacco, good health). Those figures rise by age 80. They are educational premiums from the Mejor Vida Insurance quoter (August 2026), not an offer.

Can you buy after 85?

Sometimes, with fewer companies and lower amounts. See life insurance for seniors over 85. If no company will issue new coverage, the remaining options are usually personal savings or a policy already in force.

What about after 90?

In practice, Mejor Vida Insurance appointed final expense companies do not open new applications at that age. A policy you already own does not “expire” just because you have a birthday: permanent whole life continues if premiums are paid.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a legal maximum age to buy life insurance?

There is no single nationwide cap. Each product publishes its issue ages.

Can I still buy at 80?

Yes, in many cases. See life insurance over 80. Amount and price change with age and health.

Is term available at 75?

Some shorter terms yes, others no. A 30-year term usually cuts off much earlier. Mejor Vida Insurance confirms which product fits your age.

If I already have a policy, does it cancel at a certain age?

Permanent whole life does not expire by age if you keep paying. Term ends at the end of the term period, not on a fixed birthday.

What type is most common for seniors?

For a funeral and small debts, the path Mejor Vida Insurance quotes most often is simplified-issue final expense. Term fits if you need a larger amount for a set number of years.

If I cannot buy a new policy, how is a funeral paid?

The usual options are savings, a policy already in force, or family covering the cost. Mejor Vida Insurance can check whether a product still issues at your age before you assume there is no new coverage.
