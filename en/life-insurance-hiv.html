Can you buy final expense insurance if you live with HIV?

Often there is a product, but not the one many families want first. The CDC describes HIV as a virus that attacks the immune system. With treatment, many people live for years. HIV.gov calls AIDS a late stage, when those defenses are badly damaged. On a small burial-size life policy, that medical progress does not open the health questionnaire on the products we quote.

The question How it works What matters Questions Companies Cost

Three facts to start with

  • The plan many families want first is a level plan: a policy with a short health questionnaire that can pay the full amount from the first covered payment, usually with no two-year wait.
  • With an HIV or AIDS diagnosis, that health-question plan usually cannot issue at the burial companies we quote. Then we look at a plan with no health questions, if age and amount fit.
  • We do not quote a “special HIV plan” from companies we do not appoint. We also will not claim that “no company in the country” issues a plan that pays in full. We quote what we have appointed.

The path we do quote is a guaranteed-acceptance plan — no health questions, and about a two-year wait for natural death — from ages 50 to 80, $5,000 to $25,000. Outside that age there is no automatic yes without a questionnaire on what we offer.

The worry people actually have

Families look for this coverage because a funeral, the cemetery, and small debts can fall on relatives. Final expense is permanent life insurance in a small amount, meant for that bill — it does not pay for HIV treatment and it does not replace a large income policy.

A diagnosis you already have is often called a pre-existing condition. That label does not, by itself, mean there is no insurance at all. The fear is usually: “With HIV, will they only sell me a plan that waits — or nothing?” The rest of this page explains that difference before naming companies.

What HIV and AIDS mean for your health

The CDC explains that HIV attacks the cells that help fight infection. There is no cure. Treatment — medicine a doctor prescribes — can lower the virus in the blood until a lab cannot detect it. That is called an undetectable viral load. That matters for your health and for not transmitting HIV through sex. It is not the same question a burial policy asks.

HIV.gov describes AIDS as the late stage, when the immune system is badly damaged or certain infections appear. With treatment, most people living with HIV in the United States do not reach that stage. Insurance on the products we quote does not offer a checkbox for “undetectable = the plan that pays in full.”

An insurer does not treat HIV. It decides whether the history, as it is written and as it appears in prescriptions already filled, fits a product it is willing to issue. It does not ask for a cell-count test at the agency office.

How life insurance reviews a health history

On final expense the usual path is not an office exam. There is a short questionnaire and, on most products we quote, a review of prescriptions. The NAIC reminds consumers that honest answers matter at claim time.

If those questions can be answered without hitting what that product cannot issue, the plan is usually level. If it cannot issue that way, some products pay less or return premiums in the first years. If even that cannot issue, a guaranteed-acceptance plan asks no health questions and waits about two years for natural death.

Term life at larger amounts is a different product: sometimes there are labs. An HIV diagnosis usually closes that path on what we quote. If the need is income or a mortgage, not burial, see term life with pre-existing conditions — and do not expect an automatic yes there either.

What helps, and what does not

The advantage of the no-questions plan is that the diagnosis does not close the application: there is no health questionnaire. The limitation is about a two-year wait for natural death, a smaller amount, and a higher price per dollar. Feeling well, an undetectable viral load, or many years of treatment do not open the plan that pays in full on the products we quote. Skipping the pills on a questionnaire that does ask for them does not erase prescriptions already filled.

Does not change the health-question outcome

  • An undetectable viral load.
  • Years of treatment without hospital stays.
  • Feeling well today. Insurance on these products asks the diagnosis, not how you feel.

Still matters on the no-questions plan

  • Ages 50 to 80. Outside that range there is no automatic yes without a questionnaire on what we quote.
  • An amount from $5,000 to $25,000. One policy of this kind per insured every 12 months at that company.
  • Identity, payment, and where the product is filed. The diagnosis does not replace those requirements.

HIV or AIDS

In plain language

The CDC and HIV.gov describe HIV as the virus and AIDS as a late stage. Treatment can keep the infection from reaching that stage.

What we can state

  • On Transamerica Immediate Solution, HIV or AIDS cannot issue that product.
  • On Corebridge’s product that does ask health questions, HIV or AIDS cannot issue either. The no-questions product from that company is the one we quote.

We will not state a yes or no for Living Promise or Americo Eagle Select on the diagnosis alone. We do not send a questionnaire “to see what happens” when Transamerica already cannot issue.

An undetectable viral load

In plain language

The CDC explains that treatment can lower the virus until a lab cannot detect it. That protects health and can prevent transmission through sex.

What we can state

  • That does not open Transamerica Immediate Solution. That product has no “undetectable” exception.
  • The guaranteed-acceptance plan does not ask viral load. That is why it can issue from ages 50 to 80.

HIV medicines

In plain language

HIV.gov describes treatment as the medicines that lower the virus. The insurer sees them in prescriptions already filled if the product reviews prescriptions.

What we can state

  • On Accendo, several medicines used for HIV stop that application from being completed.
  • On the guaranteed-acceptance plan there is no health questionnaire. Follow the identity and payment instructions. Do not lie on any form that does ask for medications.

We do not publish a public pill-by-pill list. List the names at the quote.

Ages 50 to 80

In plain language

The no-questions plan we quote does not select by health. It does select by age.

What we can state

  • It issues from ages 50 to 80, $5,000 to $25,000.
  • Before 50 or after 80 there is no automatic yes without a questionnaire for this diagnosis on what we quote. We do not invent a product.

The two-year wait

In plain language

If the company asks nothing about health, it cannot pay the full amount for a natural death after only a few premiums.

What we can state

  • In the first two years, a covered natural death returns premiums paid plus contract interest — 110% of premiums, on the product we quote. It does not pay the $10,000 or $25,000.
  • A covered accident can pay the full amount from the start. Suicide has its own contract period.

Other companies or a “graded HIV plan”

In plain language

Some ads promise a health-question plan with a partial payout for this diagnosis.

What we can state

  • We do not quote an appointed partial-pay plan built for HIV.
  • We do not name companies we are not appointed with.

We will not say you can stack several no-questions policies up to a large amount. At the company we quote, one policy of this kind per insured every 12 months and a $25,000 total.

Tobacco and other diagnoses

In plain language

The no-questions plan does not adjust the price for health. Age and amount rules still apply.

What we can state

  • Tobacco does not change that product’s premium. Age, sex, and amount do.
  • Dementia, hospice, or a terminal illness do not change the questions — there are none. Age, amount, and where the product is filed still apply.

These notes are not a quote. The no-questions plan still has age, amount, and state rules.

Frequently asked questions

Can they turn me down on the no-questions plan because of HIV?

Not for medical history, if you are in the product’s age and amount. You still have to meet identity, payment, and where the product is filed.

Is there a waiting period?

Yes. About two years for natural death. During that wait the family receives 110% of premiums paid, not the policy amount. A covered accident can pay the full amount from the start.

My viral load is undetectable. Does that open a health-question plan?

Not on Transamerica Immediate Solution, and not on Corebridge’s product that does ask health questions. Guaranteed acceptance remains the path from ages 50 to 80.

Is there guaranteed acceptance with no wait?

Not at the companies we quote. Zero questions and a full natural-death benefit from day one is not a product we offer.

I am 45. What is available?

The no-questions plan starts at 50. Before that we do not quote an automatic yes without a questionnaire for this diagnosis. Talk with Mejor Vida Insurance; we do not invent a product.

Do I list the pills on the no-questions plan?

That product has no health questionnaire. Follow the identity and payment instructions. Do not lie on any form that does ask for medications.

Does the policy pay if the cause is HIV or AIDS?

After the wait, this is life insurance: a check for a covered death. In the first two years, a covered natural death returns premiums plus contract interest, not the full amount.

I take pills so I do not get HIV, and I do not have HIV. Is this page for me?

No. Prevention when there is no diagnosis is a different conversation. This page is for someone who already lives with HIV or AIDS. We will not invent how a burial product treats that prevention.
