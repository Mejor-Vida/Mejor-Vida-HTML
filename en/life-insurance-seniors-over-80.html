Burial and life insurance for seniors over 80

After 80, choices narrow, but many people can still get a small whole life policy. Age, health, and tobacco drive the price. This page explains what is usually available and shows illustrative monthly premiums from companies appointed with Mejor Vida Insurance.

Updated Aug. 2026
Cost No waiting period Companies Over 85

Key points

  • If you answer the health questions and qualify, some plans can pay the full benefit from day one — even with prior medical conditions.
  • The clear recommendation is final expense insurance of $5,000 to $25,000 for a funeral, cremation, and leftover bills.
  • Many appointed companies take new applications through age 85. A few go higher; after 85 the list gets shorter.

What options do seniors over 80 have?

At this age, the fit is usually final expense insurance: a small whole life policy for the funeral. If the health questions cannot be answered, the other realistic path is guaranteed acceptance, with a wait.

  • Final expense (burial) insurance. This is a small whole life policy, usually $5,000 to $25,000. There is almost never a medical exam. There are health questions. If you qualify for a level plan, coverage can start without the two-year wait that guaranteed-acceptance plans add.
  • Guaranteed acceptance. No exam and no health questions. Approval is nearly certain within the product’s age and amount limits. In return there is almost always a two-year wait for a natural death: if death happens in that window, the family usually receives premiums paid plus contract interest, not the full benefit. It often costs more than a level plan. This is the backup if you do not qualify for level.

Keep this in mind

If health is fragile — recent cancer treatment, serious heart trouble, or other major conditions — a final expense plan (sometimes graded) is usually the realistic path. Mejor Vida Insurance compares appointed companies for the amount the family actually needs, not a large policy.

How much does burial insurance cost after 80?

With Mejor Vida Insurance appointed companies, a level $10,000 policy at age 80 is often near $95 a month for a woman and $133 a month for a man (non-tobacco, good health). The price rises with each year of age at issue. These figures are educational, not an offer.

Price changes with age, sex, tobacco, health, and amount. A plan with a two-year wait can look “easier,” but it often costs more and covers less at first.

Can seniors over 80 get coverage with no waiting period?

Yes, if you answer the health questions honestly and the company approves a level plan. “No waiting period” does not mean “no questions.” Be careful with sites that promise instant approval for everyone: that is usually guaranteed acceptance with a two-year wait.

Offers that are usually worth skipping

There are clearer — and often cheaper — paths than these:

  • Mail-only “guaranteed approval.” These almost always add a two-year wait and a high price. There is no government program that pays for a funeral for you.
  • Buying from one company without comparing. A carrier can have a familiar ad and still not be the best fit for your age, health, or state. Mejor Vida Insurance compares appointed companies for you.
  • Skipping the health questions when you can still answer them. If you qualify for a level plan, it usually costs less and can avoid the two-year wait.

Can you get life insurance after 85?

Yes, in some cases. Mutual of Omaha Living Promise Level and Transamerica Immediate Solution take new applications through 85. Aetna Accendo Level can issue through age 89 (the maximum amount drops with age). The dedicated guide covers waiting periods, licensed states, and when to call Mejor Vida Insurance.

Frequently asked questions

Will I need a medical exam after 80?

On appointed final expense plans, usually not. There is a health questionnaire. Guaranteed-acceptance plans skip the questions but add a two-year wait for a natural death.

How much coverage is usually enough?

Many families choose $10,000 to $25,000 to help with a funeral or cremation and a few bills. A traditional U.S. burial often costs more than $8,000. A free quote can size the amount.

Do premiums go up each year after the policy is issued?

On most level final expense whole life policies, no — if you keep the policy in force. What does go up is the price of a new policy if you wait more years to apply.

Can I get coverage if I already have health problems?

Sometimes yes, on a level or graded plan, depending on the answers. If no level company can issue, guaranteed acceptance is still a path, with a two-year wait.
