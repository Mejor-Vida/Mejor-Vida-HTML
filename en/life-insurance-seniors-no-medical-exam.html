Life insurance for seniors with no medical exam

For most seniors, “no medical exam” does not mean “no questions.” It usually means simplified issue: you answer health questions, and if you qualify, many final expense policies can pay the full benefit from the first premium.

Written by Julie Braunsroth, licensed insurance agent Updated Aug. 2026
What it means Types Qualify Age limit

Key points

  • Most final expense plans Mejor Vida Insurance quotes do not use an in-office medical exam. They use health questions.
  • If you qualify for a level or immediate plan, the full benefit can apply from day one (subject to contract exclusions).
  • Guaranteed acceptance is also no-exam — and almost always adds a two-year wait for natural death.

What “no medical exam” means

In senior life insurance, “no medical exam” almost never means the insurer ignores your health. It means you do not have to go to a clinic for blood or urine. You answer questions, list medications, and the company may check databases.

That is called simplified issue. It is the usual path for final expense or burial coverage. Mejor Vida Insurance compares these options among appointed companies.

Simplified issue vs. guaranteed acceptance

Both paths skip the in-office exam. The difference is the questionnaire and the wait:

  • Simplified issue. There are health questions. If the answers qualify for a level plan, there is often no two-year wait for a covered natural death.
  • Guaranteed acceptance. Few or no health questions. Approval is nearly certain within the product’s age and amount limits. In return there is almost always a two-year wait for natural death: in that window the family typically receives premiums paid plus contract interest, not the full face amount.

Keep this in mind

Sites that promise “approval for everyone, no questions” are usually guaranteed acceptance. That can be a useful Plan B. It is not the first path if you can still qualify for a plan without the extra wait.

Who usually qualifies?

You do not need perfect health. Many people with common conditions — high blood pressure, controlled diabetes, cholesterol — still qualify for a level plan with one company. The work is finding a carrier that will accept your mix of age, medications, and history.

  • Issue age and the amount you ask for
  • Tobacco or nicotine
  • Medications and recent history (cancer, heart, hospital stays)

How to check for a waiting period

Ask for the table of net death benefit by policy year. If year 1 shows the full face amount for natural death, that is immediate coverage. If it shows a percentage or “premiums plus interest,” there is a wait or a graded benefit. See also no-waiting-period coverage.

Frequently asked questions

Does life insurance for seniors always require a medical exam?

No. Most final expense plans Mejor Vida Insurance quotes use health questions, not an in-office exam.

If there is no exam, am I automatically approved?

Not necessarily. With simplified issue the company may offer immediate coverage, a graded plan, or only guaranteed acceptance, based on your answers.

Until what age can I buy coverage with no exam?

It depends on the company and the product. Many final expense plans take new applications through age 85. Some appointed companies issue later. See the age limit for buying insurance.

Can term life also skip the exam?

Some simplified or accelerated term products skip the in-office exam if you qualify. Age and face-amount limits are usually tighter than on final expense.
