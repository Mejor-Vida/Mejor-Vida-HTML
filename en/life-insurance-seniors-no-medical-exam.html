Life insurance for seniors with no medical exam

Insurers do not always require an in-office exam. For most seniors, “no exam” means a health questionnaire — and if you qualify, a final expense policy that can pay the full benefit from the first premium.

What it means Types Cost Companies FAQ

Key points

  • Most final expense plans Mejor Vida Insurance quotes do not use an in-office medical exam. They use health questions.
  • If you qualify for a level or immediate plan, the full benefit can apply from day one (subject to contract exclusions).
  • Guaranteed acceptance is also no-exam, and almost always adds a two-year wait for natural death.

A no-exam plan fits well when you need burial or final-expense coverage quickly at amounts appointed companies will issue without lab work — not when you need a very large term policy.

What “no medical exam” means

It does not mean the insurer ignores your health. It means you do not have to go to a clinic for blood or urine. You answer questions, list medications, and the company may check medical and prescription databases.

That is called simplified issue. It is the usual path for final expense or burial coverage. Mejor Vida Insurance compares these options among appointed companies. Approval is not automatic: based on your answers, you may be offered immediate coverage, a graded plan, or only guaranteed acceptance.

Some term products are also quoted without an in-office exam if the company accelerates underwriting. Age and face-amount limits are usually tighter than on final expense. If you need term, use the term life quote.

Types of no-exam life insurance for seniors

These are the paths Mejor Vida Insurance actually quotes. We do not publish products we do not appoint.

1) Final expense insurance

This is whole life meant for a funeral, cremation, small debts, and last expenses. Face amounts usually run from a few thousand dollars up to about $50,000, depending on the company. Premiums do not rise with age if you keep the policy in force. A level plan asks health questions; if you qualify, there is often no two-year wait for a covered natural death.

2) Guaranteed acceptance

Few or no health questions. Approval is nearly certain within the product’s age and amount limits. In return there is a two-year wait for natural death: in that window the family typically receives premiums paid plus contract interest, not the full face amount. It usually costs more per dollar of coverage. It is a Plan B, not the first path if you can still qualify for a level plan.

3) Term life insurance

Coverage lasts a set term (10, 20, or 30 years, or to a stated age). Some appointed products accelerate underwriting and skip the lab if you qualify. Term is not the typical burial product after 70: the term ends, and the face amount is often larger than a funeral requires. Confirm age, tobacco, and term length on the term quote.

4) Permanent whole life

Final expense is a type of whole life: it lasts a lifetime, can build cash value, and the premium stays level if you pay on time. Larger whole life amounts sometimes need more underwriting. For most seniors covering a funeral, the product we quote is simplified final expense, not a six-figure whole life policy.

Keep this in mind

Sites that promise “approval for everyone, no questions” are usually guaranteed acceptance. That can be useful. It is not the first path if you can still qualify for a plan without that extra wait.

Can you get no exam and no waiting period?

Yes, if you answer the questions honestly and the company places you on a level or immediate plan. Ask for the net death benefit by policy year: if year 1 shows the full face amount for natural death, there is no two-year wait. See also no-waiting-period coverage.

How much no-exam life insurance for seniors costs

Price depends on policy type, age, sex, tobacco, health, state, and amount. On appointed companies, a level $10,000 policy at age 65 is often near $41 a month for a woman and $54 a month for a man (non-tobacco, good health). Pros and cons

Pros

  • Speed. No lab appointment. Many final expense applications finish in minutes or a few business days.
  • Common conditions. High blood pressure, controlled diabetes, or cholesterol do not automatically close a level plan.
  • Day-one benefit if you qualify for an immediate plan (subject to the contract).
  • Convenience. You can quote by phone or online with Mejor Vida Insurance.

Cons

  • Not automatic approval. The questionnaire and databases can lead to a graded plan or only guaranteed acceptance.
  • Lower face amounts. No-exam final expense usually tops out near $25,000 to $50,000, not hundreds of thousands.
  • Guaranteed acceptance costs more per dollar and adds a two-year wait.
  • You must answer health questions honestly. An omission can affect a claim in the early years.

Is no-exam life insurance right for you?

It often fits if you want to cover a funeral or small debts, prefer not to do bloodwork, and can answer a questionnaire. If you need a very large amount, fully underwritten term can cost less per dollar — and sometimes does require an exam. If health will not pass a level plan, guaranteed acceptance remains an option, with a wait.

  • You need coverage soon for final expenses, not a 30-year term.
  • Health conditions that would make a full exam harder, but you can still pass a questionnaire.
  • You want a level whole-life premium, not term that expires.

Who usually qualifies?

You do not need perfect health. Many people with high blood pressure, controlled diabetes, or cholesterol still qualify for a level plan with one company. The work is finding a carrier that will accept your mix of age, medications, and history.

  • Issue age and the amount you ask for
  • Tobacco or nicotine
  • Medications and recent history (cancer, heart, hospital stays)

Frequently asked questions

Does life insurance for seniors always require a medical exam?

No. Most final expense plans Mejor Vida Insurance quotes use health questions, not an in-office exam.

If there is no exam, am I automatically approved?

Not necessarily. With simplified issue the company may offer immediate coverage, a graded plan, or only guaranteed acceptance, based on your answers.

Until what age can I buy coverage with no exam?

It depends on the company and the product. Many final expense plans take new applications through age 85. Some appointed companies issue later. See the age limit for buying insurance.

Can term life also skip the exam?

Some simplified or accelerated term products skip the in-office exam if you qualify. Age and face-amount limits are usually tighter than on final expense.

Is no-exam coverage more expensive than a policy with an exam?

Sometimes, especially compared with a large fully underwritten term policy. For senior final expense, the usual path already skips the lab; the bigger price jump is usually moving from a level plan to guaranteed acceptance.

Can I get no exam and no two-year wait?

Yes, if you qualify for a level or immediate plan. Guaranteed acceptance almost always includes that wait for natural death.
