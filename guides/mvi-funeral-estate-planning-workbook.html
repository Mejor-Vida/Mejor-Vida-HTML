A free gift for your family

Funeral wishes and papers workbook

Use this workbook to write, by hand, what your family will need when you die — and, if it comes to that, if you cannot make decisions for yourself.

It is not a will, a power of attorney, or a contract with a funeral home. Print it, fill it in, and give copies to people you trust. Tell them where the original is kept.

How to use it

  • Fill it in with a pen. If you change your mind later, cross out the old answer, write the new one with the date, and give out fresh copies.
  • Do not write passwords or full credit-card numbers. Name the bank or website, the last four digits if you need to tell accounts apart, and who can open your password manager.
  • You can record funeral wishes here without paying a funeral home now. Prepaying is a separate decision.
