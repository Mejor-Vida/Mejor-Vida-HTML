Privacy Policy

At Mejor Vida Insurance LLC, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, date of birth, gender, zip code, contact information (phone and email), and data related to your insurance preferences and health.

Use of Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide you with personalized final expense insurance quotes.
  • Contact you by phone, email, SMS, or WhatsApp to offer insurance options and personalized assistance.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Consent for Communications

By submitting forms on this site, you authorize Mejor Vida Insurance LLC to contact you by phone, email, SMS text messages, and WhatsApp about insurance options. For SMS, you may opt out by replying STOP. To stop WhatsApp messages, you may contact us or cease using that channel as described below.

Data Protection

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Sharing Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with authorized service providers who help us operate our business, always under strict confidentiality obligations.

User Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, please contact us via email at Julie@mejorvidainsurance.com or call 402-440-5438.

SMS, WhatsApp, and Text Message Communications

Mejor Vida Insurance LLC uses SMS text messaging and WhatsApp (along with email and calls) to communicate with clients about personalized quotes, appointment reminders, policy updates, and important coverage information.

Service Providers (SMS and WhatsApp)

We use Twilio or other providers for SMS as applicable. WhatsApp messages are sent through WhatsApp (Meta) / WhatsApp Business according to our setup and WhatsApp’s terms and policies.

How You Opt In to SMS

You agree to SMS and WhatsApp communications (plus email and calls as applicable) when you provide your mobile number and submit a quote form, contact form, or equivalent lead form on this website (or other express consent we document). Checking the consent box when shown is part of this process. Where prohibited by law, consent is not a condition of purchasing insurance.

Message Frequency

SMS is typically sent 1–5 times per week during quoting and follow-up, as needed. WhatsApp message frequency varies based on the conversation thread and case updates.

How to Opt Out

  • SMS: Reply STOP to any text message sent from our SMS number to opt out of SMS.
  • WhatsApp: Ask us in the same chat to stop WhatsApp messages, or email Julie@mejorvidainsurance.com or call 402-440-5438.
  • All channels: Julie@mejorvidainsurance.com · 402-440-5438.

Message and Data Rates May Apply

Standard message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier for SMS. WhatsApp use may involve mobile data or Wi‑Fi according to your plan and WhatsApp/Meta policies. Mejor Vida Insurance LLC does not charge a separate fee for SMS.

Privacy of Phone Numbers

SMS and WhatsApp communications are handled with safeguards consistent with your personal information. We do not sell your phone number and do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing. WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp/Meta privacy policies.

TCPA and Regulatory Compliance

Mejor Vida Insurance LLC complies with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and applicable Nebraska regulations for SMS text messaging where applicable. WhatsApp communications are also subject to WhatsApp/Meta terms and policies in addition to our practices described here.

Mejor Vida Insurance LLC is registered with the Nebraska Department of Insurance (producer license #21695431) and complies with applicable TCPA and Nebraska regulations regarding SMS and related communications.
