# FE next-24 guides — review flags

Facts that were **not** silently filled in. Editorial should confirm or keep the caveat on the page.

## Funeral costs

- NFDA **2023** national medians are used: **$8,300** (viewing + burial) and **$6,280** (viewing + cremation) on the funeral-home side. No later official NFDA median was treated as published.
- There is **no official Nebraska-only NFDA split**. Nebraska pages tell the reader to ask for the local General Price List (FTC Funeral Rule).
- A commonly cited “with vault ≈ $9,995” figure is **not** used. The existing Mejor Vida funeral-cost guide already declined to publish that extra total.

## Social Security and Medicare

- Lump-sum death payment remains **$255** on SSA’s public page. Proposed legislation to raise it is **not law**; pages note that and keep $255.
- Medicare is described as **not designed to pay for funerals** (medicare.gov). No invented funeral benefit.

## Premiums

- Illustrative **$10,000** monthly figures are from appointed-company educational tables as of **August 2026**, non-tobacco, good health (level/immediate low). They are labeled as examples, not offers.
- **No smoker multiplier** is published. Tobacco is described as a higher price class only.
- **No “cheapest company”** ranking (including Mutual of Omaha vs American Amicable).

## Product rules

- Waiting-period **formulas are not mixed**: Living Promise (premiums + 10% for two years), American Amicable (30% / 70% then 100%), Accendo modified (typical 110% of earned premiums years 1–2), Corebridge GIWL (about two years, premiums + contract interest).
- American Amicable **amount caps** are not invented; comparison page says to quote.
- **No 24-hour approval** rule. Approval time is described as often days, not guaranteed.
- **No new final expense issue at age 90** is published, consistent with appointed-product materials used elsewhere on the site.
- Assurity health charts are **not** published (not in a public document we can cite at the same detail).

## Cancellations and refunds

- Pages do **not** promise that premiums are refunded on cancel. Cash value / free-look / grace period are described as contract- and state-specific.
