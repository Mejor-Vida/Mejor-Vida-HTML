## Spoken script

Hi. I’m Jhenny, the educational assistant for Mejor Vida Insurance.

If you searched how a funeral gets paid, you are probably trying to spare the family a bill that shows up in a few days. The funeral home and the cemetery still expect to be paid for what the family chose. That bill does not wait for an estate to settle. And there is no single government check that covers it.

It feels like one job: I’ll take care of the funeral. Families usually mean one of two plans, and those plans sound alike. One is to pay a funeral home now for named items in a contract. The other is to leave cash to someone you trust, with a life insurance policy that pays that person. Both feel like the funeral is already handled. They are not the same thing.

A prepaid funeral buys what that contract names at that funeral home. It is not a blank check the family can spend on travel or at a different funeral home. A life insurance policy pays the beneficiary — the person you named. That person decides whether to pay the funeral home, travel, or another bill. The policy does not reserve a service at a named funeral home. The difference shows up if the family moves, if they want a simpler service, or if nobody can find the papers that weekend and they pay twice. Combining savings with one of those plans is normal. What goes wrong is treating the funeral-home contract as if it were insurance, or treating the insurance check as if it reserved the funeral.

People also count on Medicare or Social Security to close the gap. Medicare is medical insurance for a living person. It does not pay the funeral. Social Security may pay 255 dollars, one time, if a spouse or children meet its rules. You have to apply. That is not a typical funeral. In 2023, the National Funeral Directors Association published a median of 8,300 dollars for a funeral with a visitation and burial, on the funeral-home side. So 255 dollars and a visitation funeral are not the same size of bill.

So the funeral home still has to be paid. A prepaid contract and a life-insurance check are different kinds of money. Medicare and 255 dollars do not close a visitation funeral.
