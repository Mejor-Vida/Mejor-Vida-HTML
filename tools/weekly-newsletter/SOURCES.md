# Reputable sources — weekly newsletter

Search **the last 7 days** only (see HOW_TO_ASSEMBLE date window). Prefer primary facts you can verify.

## Allowed publishers (prefer these)

### Government and official

- FTC Funeral Rule and consumer alerts — `consumer.ftc.gov`, `ftc.gov`  
- SSA survivors / lump-sum death payment — `ssa.gov`  
- BLS CPI / prices families feel — `bls.gov`  
- State insurance departments (any U.S. state; NE, KS, CO, NV are a tie-breaker) — `*.gov`  
- NAIC consumer pages — `content.naic.org`, `naic.org`

### Associations and research

- NFDA funeral/cremation statistics — `nfda.org`  
- Insurance Information Institute — `iii.org`  
- LIMRA / Life Happens consumer research — `limra.com`, `lifehappens.org`  
- ACLI consumer education — `acli.com`

### Newsrooms (U.S.)

AP, Reuters, NPR, PBS, Wall Street Journal, New York Times, Washington Post, USA Today, Chicago Tribune, Omaha World-Herald, Denver Post, Las Vegas Sun / Review-Journal, Wichita Eagle / Kansas outlets.

Trade press (Insurance Journal, ThinkAdvisor, National Underwriter) is **discovery only**. Rewrite for families. Never paste producer framing.

## Search queries (English discovery)

Use Google News `when:7d` or equivalent. Machine list: `sources.json`.

- `"final expense insurance"` OR `"burial insurance"` OR `"funeral insurance"`  
- `"whole life insurance"`  
- `"term life insurance"`  
- `"indexed universal life"` OR IUL insurance (consumer, not illustration software)  
- annuity OR annuities (life/retirement **income**, not crypto)  
- funeral OR burial cost NFDA OR FTC "funeral rule"  
- life insurance scam OR "fake agent" (consumer protection)

Spanish queries are optional extras (`seguro de gastos finales`, `seguro de vida a término`, `anualidad`).

## Hard reject

- Medicare / Medigap / Advantage / Part D shopping  
- Captive or DTC **sales** pieces whose only point is to name Ethos, PolicyGenius, Colonial Penn, Mutual of Omaha Direct, SelectQuote, etc.  
- International-only insurance news  
- Agent commission grids, IMO trips, carrier contracting  
- Unknown blogs, Medium, Reddit, TikTok roundups as the **only** source  
- Any story whose facts you cannot attach to a working URL

## Google News note

Google News RSS is used to **find** headlines, dates, and publisher names. The newsletter must be **original** family language. Prefer the publisher’s own URL from the `<source url>` field when you can. Do not paste article text from paywalled pages.
