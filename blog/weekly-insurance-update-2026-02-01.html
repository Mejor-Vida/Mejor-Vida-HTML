1 NAIC Life Insurance Policy Locator Helps Consumers Connect with $13 Billion in Benefits

Key Statistics:
  • $13+ billion in life insurance and annuity benefits located since November 2016
  • 1.17 million policy search requests received
  • 611,000+ successful matches
  • $3 billion increase in located benefits since August 2024

The National Association of Insurance Commissioners' free online Life Insurance Policy Locator tool has achieved a significant milestone, helping consumers find over $13 billion in life insurance and annuity benefits. This represents a $3 billion increase from just one year ago.

Why This Matters for Your Family

If you've lost track of a loved one's life insurance policy, the NAIC's free tool can help you locate it. Many families don't realize benefits are waiting to be claimed. This underscores the importance of keeping beneficiaries informed about existing policies.

Source: National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC) | January 30, 2026

2 Life Insurance Market Projected to Reach $16.35 Trillion by 2033

Market Projections:
  • Market size: $7.59 trillion (2024) → $16.35 trillion (2033)
  • Compound annual growth rate: 8.9%
  • 102 million U.S. adults recognize they need more coverage
  • Individual life insurance premiums hit record $16.2 billion in 2024
  • Average policy face amount: $178,000 in 2025

The global life insurance market is experiencing explosive growth, driven by a significant underinsured population. In the United States alone, 50 million middle-income Americans and 54 million women have identified coverage gaps.

The rise of the independent workforce—projected to reach 90 million gig workers by 2025—is creating new demand for portable coverage options. Additionally, annuity sales reached a record $432.4 billion in 2024, signaling strong market health.

Why This Matters for Your Family

With so many Americans underinsured, now is an excellent time to review your coverage. The growing market means more options and competitive pricing for consumers seeking life insurance and final expense coverage.

Source: Astute Analytica / Globe Newswire | January 29, 2026

3 Life Insurance Application Activity Hits Record Growth, Up 22% in December 2025

Application Growth:

  • Record growth recorded for full year 2025

Life insurance application activity closed 2025 with remarkable momentum, according to the MIB Life Index. The dramatic spike in older consumers' applications indicates strong demand across all age groups.

Why This Matters for Your Family

Consumers are increasingly recognizing the importance of life insurance protection. If you've been putting off getting coverage, you're not alone—but this trend shows that more families are taking action to protect their loved ones.

Source: Insurance News Net / MIB Life Index | January 28, 2026

4 NAIC Adopts Revisions to Life Insurance Illustration Guidelines (AG 49-A)

Key Changes (Effective April 1, 2026):
  • Prohibits comparing historical averages with maximum illustrated rates
  • Limits historical period reporting to the most recent 25 years
  • Requires specific definitions for 'historical period,' 'inception date,' and 'index'
  • Increases uniformity in disclosure practices

The National Association of Insurance Commissioners adopted critical revisions to Actuarial Guideline XLIX-A that will significantly impact how indexed universal life (IUL) policies are illustrated and sold.

Why This Matters for Your Family

These regulatory changes aim to enhance consumer protection by preventing potentially misleading illustrations. When shopping for IUL policies, you can expect clearer, more transparent presentations of potential returns.

Source: Sidley Austin / NAIC | January 27, 2026

5 Final Expense Insurance Premiums Increased 16% in 2024, Policies Up 10%

Final Expense Market Statistics (2024):
  • New annualized premiums: $1.05 billion (up 16%)
  • Policies sold: 1.06 million (up 10%)
  • Simplified-issue average face amount: $14,535
  • Guaranteed-issue average face amount: $9,786
  • Independent distribution: 86% of policies sold
  • 75% of carriers anticipate industry-wide growth in 2025

The U.S. final expense life insurance market demonstrated exceptional strength in 2024. Nearly 60% of participating companies reported sales growth exceeding 10%, with over 30% experiencing growth of more than 25%.

Simplified-issue policies dominate the market at 85% of sales, while guaranteed-issue policies make up 15%. The strong presence of independent distribution channels (86%) highlights the value of working with independent agents who can shop multiple carriers.

Why This Matters for Your Family

Final expense insurance is more accessible than ever, with average policy amounts between $9,786 and $14,535. Working with an independent agent gives you access to multiple carriers and the best rates for your situation. Get a free quote to see your options.

Source: LIMRA / Life Insurers Council | January 27, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions

How big is the life insurance market expected to grow by 2033?

The global life insurance market is projected to reach $16.35 trillion by 2033, growing from $7.59 trillion in 2024 at an 8.9% compound annual growth rate (CAGR), according to Astute Analytica research published in January 2026.

How much did final expense insurance premiums grow in 2024?

Final expense insurance new annualized premiums grew 16% year-over-year in 2024, reaching $1.05 billion. Policy sales also increased 10% to 1.06 million policies, according to the LIMRA-Life Insurers Council Final Expense Survey.

What is the NAIC Life Insurance Policy Locator?

The NAIC Life Insurance Policy Locator is a free online tool that helps consumers find lost life insurance policies and annuities. Since launching in November 2016, it has helped locate over $13 billion in benefits, with more than 611,000 matches from 1.17 million search requests.

How much did life insurance applications increase in December 2025?

Life insurance application activity surged 22% in December 2025 according to the MIB Life Index. Notably, older consumers' life application activity was 67.5% higher in December 2025 compared to December 2024.

What is the average face amount of a final expense insurance policy?

The average face amount for simplified-issue final expense policies is $14,535, while guaranteed-issue policies average $9,786. Simplified-issue policies account for 85% of final expense sales.

What changes are coming to life insurance illustrations in 2026?

The NAIC adopted revisions to Actuarial Guideline XLIX-A (AG 49-A), effective April 1, 2026, which prohibit comparing historical averages with maximum illustrated rates and limit historical period reporting to the most recent 25-year timeframe for indexed universal life (IUL) illustrations.
