1 DOL Intensifies Enforcement on Evidence of Insurability Practices in Group Life Insurance

Key Enforcement Concerns:
  • Evidence of Insurability (EOI) forms not properly processed by some employers
  • Employees not informed of coverage limitations when EOI is pending
  • Multiple settlement agreements requiring payment of previously denied claims
  • Increased scrutiny on employer communication practices

The Department of Labor has increased its focus on evidence of insurability (EOI) practices in employer-sponsored group life insurance plans. DOL investigations have uncovered instances where employees believed they had full coverage, only to have claims denied because EOI requirements weren't properly communicated or processed.

Common issues identified include employers failing to submit EOI forms in a timely manner, inadequate communication about coverage limitations while EOI is pending, and inconsistent follow-up on incomplete applications. These enforcement actions have resulted in settlement agreements requiring insurers to pay previously denied claims.

Why This Matters for Your Family

If you have group life insurance through your employer, verify your coverage status. Don't assume you're fully covered—ask HR to confirm that any required evidence of insurability forms have been approved. Consider supplementing group coverage with individual life insurance for guaranteed protection.

Source: National Insurance Services (NIS) Blog | February 1, 2026

2 New York DFS Proposes Comprehensive Life Insurance Regulatory Updates for 2026

Proposed Regulatory Updates:
  • Updated life insurance illustration requirements
  • Enhanced consumer protection measures
  • New guidelines for accelerated underwriting using data analytics
  • Continued cybersecurity requirements for insurers
  • Focus on suitability standards for life insurance sales

The New York State Department of Financial Services (DFS) has released its 2026 regulatory agenda, outlining comprehensive updates affecting life insurance companies operating in New York. As one of the most influential insurance regulators in the country, New York's rules often influence standards nationwide.

The proposed updates aim to modernize regulations for the digital age while strengthening consumer protections. New guidelines for accelerated underwriting address the growing use of data analytics and algorithms in the application process, ensuring these technologies are used fairly and transparently.

Why This Matters for Your Family

Stronger consumer protections mean clearer information when shopping for life insurance. The focus on illustration requirements ensures you'll receive more accurate projections of policy performance, helping you make better-informed decisions.

Source: New York State Department of Financial Services | January 31, 2026

3 Illinois Bans Life Insurance Discrimination Based on Felony Convictions

New Law Highlights (Effective January 1, 2026):
  • Prohibits denial of life insurance solely based on criminal history
  • Bans higher premiums based on felony convictions alone
  • Prevents coverage limitations based on past convictions
  • Insurers may still assess risk factors related to specific circumstances
  • Affects an estimated 1.9 million Illinois residents with criminal records

Illinois has implemented groundbreaking legislation that prohibits life insurance companies from discriminating against applicants solely based on their criminal history or felony convictions. The law, which took effect January 1, 2026, aims to reduce barriers to financial protection for formerly incarcerated individuals and their families.

The legislation doesn't prevent insurers from conducting proper risk assessment. Companies can still consider factors directly related to an applicant's current health, lifestyle, and specific circumstances. However, they cannot use a felony conviction as an automatic disqualification or basis for charging higher rates.

Why This Matters for Your Family

This law expands access to life insurance protection for Illinois residents with criminal records. If you or a family member was previously denied coverage due to a conviction, you may now qualify for protection. Working with an independent agent can help navigate available options.

Source: Insurasales | February 1, 2026

4 Life Insurance Industry Maintains Strong Financial Position Despite Economic Headwinds

Industry Financial Indicators:
  • Stable capital levels across major life insurers
  • Record annuity sales of $432.4 billion in 2024 supporting earnings
  • Manageable commercial real estate exposure
  • Investment-grade ratings for most major insurers
  • Diversified investment portfolios weathering market volatility

According to S&P Global Ratings, the U.S. life insurance industry entered 2026 in a strong financial position, maintaining solid capital levels and earnings despite ongoing economic challenges. The continued demand for annuity products has helped offset pressures in other business lines.

While concerns about commercial real estate exposure remain, most insurers have managed their portfolios conservatively. Investment-grade ratings reflect the industry's overall stability and ability to meet policyholder obligations.

Why This Matters for Your Family

A financially healthy insurance industry means greater confidence that your life insurance policy will pay as promised. When shopping for coverage, look for insurers with strong financial ratings (A or better from AM Best) to ensure long-term security.

Source: Investment News / S&P Global Ratings | February 5, 2026

5 Major Life Insurers Integrate Wellness Programs and AI Technology to Improve Policyholder Health

Wellness Integration Features:
  • Wearable device partnerships (Fitbit, Apple Watch, etc.)
  • Health coaching apps with personalized recommendations
  • Premium discounts up to 25% for healthy behaviors
  • AI-powered risk assessment for faster underwriting
  • Rewards programs for exercise, healthy eating, preventive care

Major life insurers including John Hancock, Prudential, and others are expanding their wellness program offerings, using AI technology and wearable devices to help policyholders live healthier lives. These programs offer tangible benefits beyond the traditional death benefit.

John Hancock's Vitality program, for example, rewards policyholders with premium discounts and gift cards for healthy activities tracked through connected devices. AI algorithms analyze health data to provide personalized coaching and identify early warning signs of health issues.

Why This Matters for Your Family

Wellness-integrated life insurance can save you money while improving your health. If you use fitness trackers or focus on healthy living, these programs reward your efforts with premium discounts and additional benefits. Ask about wellness features when comparing policies.

Source: MassLive / Insurance News Net | February 7, 2026

6 LIMRA Forecasts Moderate Life Insurance Growth with Product Line Shifts in 2026-2027

LIMRA Growth Projections:
  • Total individual life premiums: 2-6% annual growth through 2027
  • Term life insurance: ~2% modest growth, impacted by middle-market conditions
  • Whole life: Slow but steady gains, shift to short-pay premiums
  • Indexed Universal Life (IUL): Normalizing after 21-25% surge in 2025
  • Variable Universal Life (VUL): Slowing from exceptional 2025 levels
  • Final expense market: Muted growth due to economic slowdown

LIMRA's 2026-2027 forecast projects moderate growth for the U.S. individual life insurance market following an exceptional 2025. Total individual life premiums grew 8-12% in 2025, driven largely by strong IUL and VUL sales, but are expected to normalize to 2-6% annual growth.

The research organization recommends carriers focus on simplified-issue and final expense products to capture middle-market demand, while managing exposure to market volatility. The estate and gift tax exemption increase to $15 million per person is expected to stabilize demand in estate planning segments.

Why This Matters for Your Family

A growing, competitive market benefits consumers. Continued product innovation and market expansion mean more options at various price points. Final expense and simplified-issue products remain focused on accessibility for middle-market families seeking affordable coverage.

Source: LOMA / LIMRA | February 1, 2026

7 NAIC Life Actuarial Task Force Considers Pension Risk Transfer Reinvestment Guardrails

Task Force Considerations:
  • Reviewing reinvestment assumptions for pension risk transfer deals
  • Focus on protecting pension beneficiaries after transfers to insurers
  • Potential new actuarial guidelines for reserve calculations
  • Meeting scheduled for February 9, 2026
  • Part of broader solvency oversight initiatives

The NAIC Life Actuarial Task Force is considering new guardrails for pension risk transfer transactions, where corporations transfer pension obligations to life insurance companies. The focus is on ensuring adequate reserves to protect the beneficiaries who depend on these transferred pension benefits.

This regulatory attention follows significant growth in pension risk transfer activity, with life insurers taking on billions of dollars in pension liabilities. The task force aims to balance enabling these transactions while ensuring long-term security for beneficiaries.

Why This Matters for Your Family

If your employer has transferred pension benefits to an insurance company, these regulatory discussions aim to protect your retirement security. Strong oversight ensures insurers maintain adequate reserves to pay future benefits as promised.

Source: AM Best | February 6, 2026
